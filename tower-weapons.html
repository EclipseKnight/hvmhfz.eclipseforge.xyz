    How Tower weapon upgrades work

    • Making one: talk to the cat with the backpack in the smithy (the Tower smithy), Tower Weapons. It costs one Tower book and no zenny; the 17 Zenith weapons (100-point budget) take an Entrusted Secret Text and one Zenith part instead. There is no rank gate at the smithy: the books are in the Hunting Road shop from GR 1, and the Zenith parts drop from Zenith ★1 breaks (GR 300).
    • Levels: "Level GR Weapons" at the same smithy raises one stat a level at a time. Each level costs materials and uses points. A weapon has a budget of 300 points (100 for the Zenith ones), so it cannot max every stat: Attack alone fills the budget of most weapons, and you trade Attack for Sharpness, Affinity, Element or Status.
    • Sigils open lines: Affinity needs an Affinity Sigil, Element a Fire, Water, Thunder, Ice or Dragon Sigil, and Status a Poison, Paralysis or Sleep Sigil, in one of the weapon's own Tower Sigil slots. The status sigil picks which column of levels the Status line uses. Sigils are made at the Tower smithy (Craft T. Sigil).
    • Materials: mostly Courage, Glittering and Divine Gems; some levels also take a Soul (Top) and a Ribbon High of the weapon's class (from their quests), or, for the Zenith weapons, Zenith break parts. Levels cannot be undone.
    • On this server: the gems are sold in bundles in the Hunting Road shop (10 a purchase) and the Diva GCP item shop (30 a purchase), and a Sky Corridor clear pays about 24 Courage, 16 Glittering and 4 Divine Gems on average (more in the 3rd and 4th District). The planner prices each build in those shops from the live shop lists. Your levels are saved with your character.

    Testers only, not on the live server: on the Test land with the pre-release patch v1.32-pre12 or later, Courage, Glittering and Divine Gems come only from the Sky Corridor (no Hunting Road or Diva rows), a clear draws about 20 rewards with a Duremudira kill and 13 to 17 without, the 2nd to 4th Districts also drop the Tower crafting materials, and those materials cost 25,000 to 65,000 RdP in the Hunting Road shop. The 2nd, 3rd and 4th District need Tower Rank 51, 100 and 200 there. The levels, points and materials per level above are the same on both.
