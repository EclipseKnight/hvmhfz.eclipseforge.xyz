Leaderboards

Who hunted the most this week, this month and of all time, from the server kill log. Weeks start Monday 00:00 UTC and months on the 1st. The Hunting Road board ranks each Road rotation by the highest floor cleared. Characters that were never played or hold corrupt records are left out.

Top hunters

Ranked by the highest floor reached in one run during the rotation, then by who reached it first. Each rotation has its own board, and so do the open and solo Roads. Floors climbed before the server started recording each clear (29 Oct, 02:19 UTC) come from each hunter's saved best: their floor shows, but their floor count and tie order do not.
