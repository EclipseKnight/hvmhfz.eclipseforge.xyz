    Leaderboards

    Who hunted the most this week, this month and of all time. The week and month boards come from the server kill log; All time is the in-game Monster List counts (slain and captured, as the game shows them). Weeks start Monday 00:00 UTC and months on the 1st. The Hunting Road board ranks each Road rotation by the highest floor cleared. The Sky Corridor board ranks hunters by tower points from cleared tower quests, or by Duremudira kills, floors or tower clears in all districts or one. Characters that were never played or hold corrupt records are left out.

    Top hunters

    Ranked by the highest floor reached in one run during the rotation, then by who reached it first. Each rotation has its own board, and so do the open and solo Roads. Every floor is recorded by the game server as it is cleared and tagged with the Road data it was climbed on, so a board shows exactly who climbed what, on which map, and when. Boards start empty at a new rotation. The Original Road ran before floors were tracked, so its board is each hunter's saved best floor and points (no date); a tie on a saved best goes to the higher points. When a Road retires, its board is frozen as the original board and everything climbed after that goes to a separate revisit board, so going back never overwrites an old record.
