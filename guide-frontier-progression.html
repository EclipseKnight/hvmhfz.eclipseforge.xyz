    Progression: HR1 to end game

    One path from your first quest to the end game, in stages. Each stage says what to aim for, what to do, which gear to make, where to find it in the game, and when you are done. Signed in? The Characters tab of your Account page shows your own next step, and /next in our Discord says the same.

    Not installed yet? Start on the Play page. Hunsterverse runs the last Japanese Frontier Z client with an English patch, so a few things differ from the original game. Where they do, this page says so with a Hunsterverse mark. For more on a system named here (Hunter Navi, Partner, Halk, Poogie, Rasta and the rest), follow its link to Game systems.

    How Hunter Rank moves on

    Hunter Rank comes in seven ranks, HR1 to HR7. Each rank covers a range of the HR the game shows:

    RankHR shownTo move on
    HR1HR 16 Key Quests, then the Urgent Quest
    HR2HR 2-3010 Key Quests, then the Urgent Quest at HR 30
    HR3HR 31-507 Key Quests, then the Urgent Quest at HR 50
    HR4HR 51-998 Key Quests, then the Urgent Quest at HR 99
    HR5HR 100-29922 Key Quests, then the Urgent Quest at HR 299
    HR6HR 300-9985 Key Quests; no Urgent Quest
    HR7HR 999The HR7 Urgent Quest takes you to G Rank
    1. Clear every Key Quest of your rank. They are in Quest Counter > Hunter Quests, in your rank's ★ list, marked with a star crest and sorted first.
    2. When every Key Quest is cleared and your HR is high enough, the rank's Urgent Quest appears in Quest Counter > Hunter Quests > Urgent Quests.
    3. Clear the Urgent Quest: your HR cap rises and the next rank starts.

    Until the Urgent Quest is cleared, your HR stops at the top of your rank (HR 30 in HR2, for example). Any quest raises your HR up to that cap. Each list below says the monster form when the quest file has one (Hardcore or Supremacy). More on each rank: Hunter Rank and Style Rank.

    First steps: your hunter and the tutorial

    Goal: make your hunter, get into town and collect everything the server gives a new hunter.

    Do this:

    1. Install the game, make your account and log in: Play. Pick a world on the world list (Worlds); any world is fine to start.
    2. Make your character. The instructor greets you in town and may send you on a short Gogomoa quest: take the coin from the blue box to the red box, then kill the Gogomoa.
    3. Open the Hunter Navi (Shift+C on a keyboard, R1+R2 on a controller) and read every task in each tab: the "read this" tasks complete at once and give potions, whetstones and cheques. Keep the cheques in your box and sell one at any box when you need zenny; each sells for the sum on it. More: Hunter Navi.
    4. Hunsterverse Take the free packs: on the Event Campaigns screen in town, the New & Returning Hunter Pack (once per character: Mega Potions, Max Potions, Mega Demondrugs and Armorskins, tickets and Tower Medals) and the Weekly Supply Drop (every Monday). The items go into your item box. More: Event quests and campaigns.
    5. Hunsterverse Claim your gifts with Claim Items at the Gacha Felyne: a few gifts are waiting for every hunter (Poogie Dressup! gives Poogie outfits 1 to 30), and 10 Trial Coin G arrive every Monday. Take the Daily Free Draw there too: 3 free draws a day. More: Gifts and Daily and weekly rewards.
    6. Set your controls and options (Options > Control Options). The basics: Basics: first login.
    7. Visit the Helper Area of your house: your Halk joins every quest and can heal, buff or attack (Halk). The Gu-ku Farm next to it gives materials once per quest.

    Gear to aim for: none yet. The starter weapon is stronger than anything you can make early on.

    Where: the Quest Counter (the guild girl in black at the middle counters of the town square) takes you to your first Key Quests.

    You are done when: you have taken your first ★1 Key Quest.

    HR1 (HR 1)

    Goal: clear the 6 Key Quests and the Urgent Quest, against Abiorugu.

    Do this: take the Key Quests from Quest Counter > Hunter Quests > ★1. Keep doing the Hunter Navi tasks as you go: most finish on the way.

    Gear to aim for: the starter weapon is enough. If you want to learn crafting, make a weapon and armour from these monsters at the Blacksmith.

    You are done when: the Urgent Quest is cleared and you are HR2.

    HR2 (HR 2-30)

    Goal: set up your helpers and clear the 10 Key Quests and the Urgent Quest (Espinas).

    Do this:

    • Carry a Powercharm (true attack +6) and an Armorcharm (defence +16) in your pouch from now on. The shop (the pink guildie) lists them at 36,000z and 24,000z: sell a cheque or two to pay, not a big stack. Hunsterverse We have not checked in game which items our shop sells; if it has no charms, skip this, it is a small bonus. More: Basics: Hunter Rank 2.
    • Make your Partner: first upgrade your house (the House icon, 10,000z), then at the Rasta bar up the town stairs talk to the Legendary Rasta with the weapon you want your Partner to use. Dual Swords or Sword and Shield work best. More: Partner.
    • Contract another player's Rasta at the bar: it fights with you and earns you GCP. More: Rasta.
    • Get your Caravan Gem: in the Caravan Area by the town entrance, talk to Kashira by the bonfire. Its first skill, Recovery Items Up, makes your potions heal more. More: Caravan.
    • Join a guild (the game says Clan) at the Guild Manager in town: leave the newbie guild first. Hunsterverse A guild meal can be eaten for 7 days after it is cooked here, and treasure hunts are free, so a guild pays off even if you play alone. Find one on Guilds or in our Discord. More: Guild.

    Gear to aim for: one Low Rank set from the monsters you hunt, kept and upgraded. Hunsterverse Since client patch v1.19 (9 Nov 2026) each upgrade level of Low and High Rank armour costs a single Armor Sphere of the right tier instead of monster parts, so upgrading the set you have is cheaper than making new ones.

    You are done when: you are HR 30, every Key Quest is cleared and the Urgent Quest is cleared (HR3).

    HR5 (HR 100-299)

    Goal: the longest rank, and where Frontier really starts. Clear the 22 Key Quests (21 of them Hardcore) and the Urgent Quest, a Supremacy Odibatorasu repel.

    Do this:

    • Claim the HR5 items from the Purple Guildie at the Town Entrance: materials for about three Zerureusu weapons.
    • Walk to the Guild Master to start Style Rank: Heaven and Storm Style for every weapon (Change Equipment > Equip Book of Secrets), SR skills and Hardcore quests. Storm Style suits most weapons. See Style Rank.
    • Learn Hardcore: turn on the HC switch on the last screen before a quest. The Key Quests below are already Hardcore.
    • The HR Exotic Quests (Quest Counter > Hunter Quests > Exotic Quests) open at HR 100: Zinogre, Nargacuga, Brachydios and the other mainline imports.
    • Hunsterverse Older guides spend GCP at HR5 on Lao-Shan Claws and wyvern materials. Our GCP shop sells Diva Defense items instead, so keep your GCP for Partner skills and the Diva shops (Currencies). Get the materials from the monsters: each item's page says where it comes from here.

    Gear to aim for: a Zerureusu weapon first, then Gou weapons and armour, which upgrade all the way into G Rank. The HR5 mixed set in Armour lasts until G Rank.

    You are done when: you are HR 299 and the Urgent Quest is cleared (HR6).

    HR7 (HR 999)

    Goal: clear the G Rank Urgent Quest and enter G Rank.

    Do this: talk to the G Rank guildie (the girl with the umbrella) to post the Urgent Quest: a Shantien repel on an airship. Take ballista shots from the box on the deck for the binders; the fight has three phases. HR7 has no Key Quests. Then switch your main weapon's SR skill to Affinity Up.

    You are done when: the Guild Master has started you in G Rank (talk to her a few times after the repel).

    G Rank milestones

    In G Rank there are no Key Quests: every G★1 to G★7 quest list is open from GR1. Your GR rises with the GR points (GRP) of any G Rank quest, up to GR 999 (13,716,950 GRP in all). These GR open the next things:

    GR100G Exotic quests (★8), for Exotic weapon upgrades and the Exotic materials, and the Musou Zinogre, Nargacuga and Deviljho quests (they ask for GR 100)G counter > G Hunter Quests > G Exotic Quests
    GR300Zenith ★1 monsters, Zenith armour and weapons, the Zenith materials in the Hunting Road shopG counter > Zenith Monster Quests

    GR700Zenith ★3 monstersZenith Monster Quests

    The Hunting Road shop gates are from our shop data. Unlimited mode, SR food and the GR999 armour store are as the original guide describes them; we have not checked them in game here.

    Hunsterverse changes. Zenith ★1, ★2, ★3 and ★4 moved from GR 200, 400, 600 and 800 to GR 300, 500, 700 and 900 on 17 November 2026, and the Zenith materials in the Hunting Road shop now need GR300. Gear you already made stays yours.

    • Until the next client patch, the 92 older Zenith quests at the quest counter still show and accept the old GR, and the smithy shows its Zenith menu from GR 200.
    • G Exotic quests open at GR 100 on our server (G counter, G Hunter Quests, G Exotic Quests), not at GR 500 as in the original game. The HR Exotic quests (HR counter, Hunter Quests, Exotic Quests) need HR 100.
    • There is no GR 800 gate for the G Finesse upgrades of G Lv50 and Exotic weapons in this client. The materials, Exotic Gem, Exotic Bone and Exotic Shl, come from the G Exotic quests since 17 November: each clear gives 1 to 4 of one of them, by monster (Gem: Zinogre, Stygian Zinogre, Barioth, Amatsu; Bone: Deviljho, Uragaan, Gore Magala, Shagaru Magala; Shl: Nargacuga, Brachydios, Seregios).
    • The 30 GRP quests (Massive GRP!, Gz & GRP Bonanza and the like) need GR 900 since 24 November, so they can no longer skip you to GR 900. The four built into the game (GRP Bonanza ★1, ★3 and ★7, Lots of GRP) still show at any GR until the next client patch.

    The stages below walk you through these milestones. More in Starting G Rank, G Rank and Zenith.

    GR 1-49: your first G Rank sets

    Goal: a full G Rank set and a G Rank weapon, and the habits G Rank needs.

    Do this:

    • Walk up to the Guild Master: every weapon you have made moves to GSR (G Style Rank) and gets Extreme Style, the best style for almost every weapon. More: GSR.
    • Follow the Hunter Navi G Rank tasks (Series 1 to 3). They lead you through the Newbie set, Gurenzeburu decorations, free weapons, then the Furogada and Samurai sets: Hunter Navigation tasks.
    • Start with G★1 to G★3 quests. A quest's stars lower your defence (−150 per star above ★1), so move up a star only when your set keeps up.
    • Take the first My Missions for about 20 Attack Ceiling levels (My Missions), and put Affinity Up on as your SR skill.

    Gear to aim for: the Newbie set, then the Furogada or Samurai set; a G8 event weapon or prep weapon (Easy equipment).

    Where: G Rank counter > G Hunter Quests > G★1 to G★7.

    You are done when: GR 50.

    Hunsterverse. All of G★1 to G★7 is open from GR 1 here (the server opens every list to everyone), so nothing stops you taking a G★7 quest on your first day. The order above is the advice, not a lock.

    GR 50-99: cuffs and the harder G★ quests

    Goal: fill your set's skills with cuffs and decorations, and move up to G★4 to G★7.

    Do this:

    • Buy the G Rank Pugi Cuff Licence (60P) from the My Tore manager, then make G cuffs: G Rank Pugi cuffs.
    • Finish Hunter Navi Series 3: Burst gear, Origin weapons and Voljang (G Rank Series 3).
    • Learn sigils and put them in your weapons (Making and using Sigils).
    • Start the Hunting Road (the H.Road/Sky Corridor counter), with a party if you can. Its Road points (RdP) stay yours and buy, among much else, decorations, Raviente materials, Tower weapon gems and, from GR 500, every weapon type's Hiden decorations (25,000 RdP each; see End game). A few runs a week now save a lot of farming later. Every row of the Road shop and its price is on Sources.

    Gear to aim for: G cuffs, G Rank decorations, Burst armour and an Origin weapon. Plan sets with the Set builder.

    Where: G Hunter Quests > G★4 to G★7; the Blacksmith; My Tore; the H.Road/Sky Corridor counter.

    You are done when: GR 100.

    GR 100-299: G Exotic and a GX set

    Goal: a GX set and strong G weapons before Zenith.

    Do this:

    • Hunt the G Exotic monsters (★8). Hunsterverse They open at GR 100 here, and every clear gives 1 to 4 Exotic Gem, Exotic Bone or Exotic Shl.
    • Use those materials to make the G versions of Exotic and event weapons and level them to G Lv50 (50 of one material per weapon): Exotic and G Finesse weapons.
    • Upgrade your G armour to GX, and make the G Class decorations (G Class decorations).
    • Try the Musou quests that open at GR 100 (Musou Zinogre, Nargacuga and Deviljho), in the G Event Quests. They are hard; a party helps.
    • Raise your Attack Ceiling with My Missions as your attack grows (about 25 levels for a basic G Rank set).

    Gear to aim for: a GX set with G cuffs and decorations, and a G Lv50 or G Finesse weapon.

    Where: G counter > G Hunter Quests > G Exotic Quests; G Event Quests; the Blacksmith.

    You are done when: GR 300.

    GR 300-899: Zenith ★1 to ★3

    Goal: your first Zenith armour and weapon, then upgrade them through the tiers.

    Do this:

    • Take the Zenith quests from Zenith Monster Quests on the G counter. ★1 opens at GR 300, ★2 at GR 500, ★3 at GR 700 Hunsterverse. Every quest in these lists is a Zenith monster (the form is in the quest file).
    • Break each monster's special part: it gives the tier's key material. Most other materials drop on every tier, so you can farm them on ★1 to ★3.
    • Get at least 55 in the matching resistance before you fight a monster with a new blight (Blights and status).
    • The Hunting Road shop sells the Zenith materials from GR 300 (G materials tab).

    Gear to aim for: Zenith armour from Z to ZX (ZX Lv1 is enough), Zenith skills, a Zenith weapon and a Zenith cuff: Zenith.

    Where: G counter > Zenith Monster Quests; the quest list (Form: Zenith).

    You are done when: GR 900.

    GR 900-999: Zenith ★4, GRP and GSR

    Goal: finish your Zenith set on ★4, reach GR 999 and raise your weapons' GSR.

    Do this:

    • Fight Zenith ★4 (GR 900 here) only for the top parts you need: about 3 to 5 kills with the part broken.
    • The 30 GRP quests (Massive GRP!, Gz & GRP Bonanza and the like) open at GR 900 here Hunsterverse. Use them to finish GR 999 and to raise the GSR of your weapons.
    • Each weapon type has its own GSR, up to 999. A weapon at GSR 999 gets Passive Master and Secret Technique; with 11 weapons at GSR 999 you also get Soul Revival (Style Rank skills).

    Gear to aim for: a finished ZX set, Zenith or Exotic weapons; start planning your end-game weapon (Tower, Raviente or Hiden, below).

    You are done when: GR 999 (2,371,050 GRP from GR 900).

    Hunsterverse. With client patch v1.30, a hunter who has a weapon in GSR mode may be checked against that weapon's GSR (900) instead of the GR on the GRP quests. A fix that checks GR only is on the Test land and comes with the next client patch. Not checked in game.

    End game: what to do at GR 999

    There is no last quest. These are the long goals; pick the ones you enjoy. Most players do several at once.

    Hunting Road. A run of floors with stronger monsters each floor. It pays Road points (RdP) for the Hunting Road shop: Road gems, decorations, cuff tickets, Zenith materials, Tower weapon books and gems, Divine Power Gems for Raviente weapons, Skill Slots Up and, at GR 999, Hiden Stamps. Hunsterverse Players design and vote on the Road, and you choose your party Road and your solo Road apart (!road, !road solo, or Game options on your Account page). Where: the H.Road/Sky Corridor counter, Hunting Road. More: Hunter's Road, Roads in game, Rotation and vote.

    Sky Corridor (the Tower). Climb floors to Duremudira for Tower Medals, the Courage, Glittering and Divine Gems that upgrade Tower weapons, Tower decorations and crafting materials. Tower points raise your Tower Rank. Hunsterverse No guild needed. The 3rd and 4th Districts are ours: Zenith bosses (Zenith ★2 and ★3 strength in the 3rd, ★4 with 25% more damage in the 4th) and better rewards. Tower > Daily Rewards gives 5 Tower Medals a day. Where: the H.Road/Sky Corridor counter; the tower quests are in its Hunting Road list on the stable patch. More: Sky Corridor.

    Tower weapons. 63 weapons made at the Tower smithy (the cat with the backpack in the smithy) from a Tower book, then levelled with gems within a points budget; the 17 Zenith ones need a Zenith part (GR 300 and up). Plan one on Tower weapons. Hunsterverse For now the Courage, Glittering and Divine Gems are also sold in bundles: in the Hunting Road shop for RdP, and in the Diva GCP shop (30 for 180 to 300 GCP), so you need not farm every gem in the tower.

    Raviente (Great Slaying). A server-wide siege for up to 32 hunters (Berserk Raviente), joined at Kieru in the Caravan Area. Hunsterverse Register at Kieru, then type !ravi start in chat to start it with any number of hunters; alone or in a small group your damage counts more, so you can finish it. It pays the gems and parts for Raviente weapons, which grow to level 100 and end in the Zenith ★4 line. The Hunting Road shop also sells Divine Power Gems and Raviente parts. More: Raviente and Raviente in Game systems.

    Hiden. A weapon type's Hiden skill is the top of its Tech skill (30 points). Hunsterverse You do not need the old grind of Souls, Ribbons and Merits for it: the Hunting Road shop's Decorations tab sells each weapon type's four Hiden decorations (for example SnS Hiden· White, Red, Black and Blue: Tech +6 and one more skill each, one slot) for 25,000 RdP each, from GR 500; the four True decorations (Tech +6 and a stronger second skill) cost 50,000 RdP each, from GR 900 (hunters below the rank do not see the rows). Five of them give the 30 points, so a full Hiden skill is 125,000 RdP; fewer if your armour has Tech points. Plan it with the Set builder. The old way: Hiden decorations.

    Unlimited (UL) quests. At GR 999, quests with a blue flame can be switched to UL: Zenith ★3 to ★4 strength, with weapon Up sigils and double Hiden materials (GR999 and Unlimited Mode). Hunsterverse UL clears also give Hiden Stamps.

    Musou and Upper Shiten. The hardest event fights; each clear has a 2% chance of a Prayer Stone here Hunsterverse. Find them with the quest list (Form: Musou).

    Attack Ceiling. Keep raising it with My Missions: about 80 to 130 levels for Hiden and Determination sets (Attack Ceiling).

    Events. Hunsterverse The event calendar runs two lanes, one after another: the Hunter Festival and Diva Defense, and the weekly Speedrun Cup and the VS Bracket; MezFes is open every day. Each event ends with a gift (GW Lottery Tickets and Trial Coin G). See Events, Tournaments and Regular Events.

    Every day. 3 free draws at the Gacha Cat (Daily Free Draw), 5 Tower Medals from the Sky Corridor's Daily Rewards, and My Tore Points. Every Monday: 10 trial gacha coins and the Weekly Supply Drop on the Event Campaigns screen (Currencies). None of it piles up into a chore: skip a day and nothing is lost but that day's draw.
