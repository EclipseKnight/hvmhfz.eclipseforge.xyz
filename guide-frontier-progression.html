    Progression: HR1 to Zenith

    Every Key Quest and Urgent Quest from HR1 to HR7, then the G Rank milestones up to Zenith ★4. Signed in? The Characters tab of your Account page shows your own next step, and /next in our Discord says the same.

    How Hunter Rank moves on

    Hunter Rank comes in seven ranks, HR1 to HR7. Each rank covers a range of the HR the game shows:

    RankHR shownTo move on
    HR1HR 16 Key Quests, then the Urgent Quest
    HR2HR 2-3010 Key Quests, then the Urgent Quest at HR 30
    HR3HR 31-507 Key Quests, then the Urgent Quest at HR 50
    HR4HR 51-998 Key Quests, then the Urgent Quest at HR 99
    HR5HR 100-29922 Key Quests, then the Urgent Quest at HR 299
    HR6HR 300-9985 Key Quests; no Urgent Quest
    HR7HR 999The HR7 Urgent Quest takes you to G Rank
    1. Clear every Key Quest of your rank. They are marked with a star in the quest lists and sort first.
    2. When every Key Quest is cleared and your HR is high enough, the rank's Urgent Quest appears in Quest Counter > Hunter Quests > Urgent Quests.
    3. Clear the Urgent Quest: your HR cap rises and the next rank starts.

    Until the Urgent Quest is cleared, your HR stops at the top of your rank (HR 30 in HR2, for example). Any quest raises your HR up to that cap. More on each rank: Hunter Rank and Style Rank.

    HR7 (HR 999)

    HR7 has no Key Quests. Its Urgent Quest is the fight with Shantien on an airship (named from the guide), and clearing it takes you into G Rank. How the fight works: Starting G Rank.

    G Rank milestones

    In G Rank there are no Key Quests: every G★1 to G★7 quest list is open from GR1. Your GR rises with the GR points of any G Rank quest, and these GR open the next things:

    GRWhat opensIn the game now
    GR50The G Rank Pugi Cuff Licence (60P from the Pugi Farm Manager), to wear G Rank cuffsGR50
    GR100G Exotic quests (★8), for Exotic weapon upgrades, and the Musou Armor Boost questsGR100 (every playable G Exotic quest)
    GR300Zenith ★1 monsters, and Zenith armour and weaponsGR300 (since 17 Nov)
    GR500Zenith ★2 monstersGR500 (since 17 Nov)
    GR700Zenith ★3 monstersGR700 (since 17 Nov)
    GR900Zenith ★4 monsters, the hardest standard fights in the gameGR900 (since 17 Nov)

    Changed on 17 November 2026. Zenith ★1, ★2, ★3 and ★4 moved from GR 200, 400, 600 and 800 to GR 300, 500, 700 and 900, and the Zenith materials in the Hunting Road shop now need GR300. Gear you already made stays yours.

    • The 92 older Zenith quests at the quest counter still show the old GR in the game until the next client patch.
    • G Exotic quests open at GR 100 on our server (G counter, G Hunter Quests, G Exotic Quests), not at GR 500 as in the original game. They were GR 500 at first and moved to GR 100 with the Zenith release, because Zenith started at GR 200. The HR Exotic quests (HR counter, Hunter Quests, Exotic Quests) need HR 100.
    • There is no GR 800 gate for the G Finesse upgrades of G Lv50 and Exotic weapons in this client. The materials, Exotic Gem, Exotic Bone and Exotic Shl, come from the G Exotic quests since 17 November: each clear gives 1 to 4 of one of them, by monster (Gem: Zinogre, Stygian Zinogre, Barioth, Amatsu; Bone: Deviljho, Uragaan, Gore Magala, Shagaru Magala; Shl: Nargacuga, Brachydios, Seregios).

    What to build on the way: the Furogada or Samurai set from G★1-3 (the Hunter Navigation tasks lead you through it), a GX set with G cuffs and decorations before Zenith, then Zenith gear. More in Starting G Rank, G Rank and Zenith.
