Roads in game

Which Hunting Road a quest room runs, how a room picks one, and what happens to everyone else in the party.

A Road is the map the Hunting Road is built on. The server knows every Road it has run by its hash, so a room can play an old one without touching the records it already earned.

1. What a Road is

Hunting Road is the tower climb. The Road is the set of maps and monster pools it is built from, and the server tracks each one by a hash. Rotations change the live Road every two weeks, but every Road the server has run stays playable and keeps its own board.

A room can be pinned to any Road the server has installed. The pin belongs to the room, not to the server, so two parties can climb different Roads at the same time.

2. Set your default Road on the site

Open your account page, the Account tab, panel Road you play, or the same panel at the top of the Road builder. Pick a Road and press Save Road. The server pins that Road when you open a quest room; choosing Follow the live Road leaves the room unpinned.

Your default only applies to rooms you host. A guest cannot pin their own choice onto somebody else's room.

3. Choose a Road in the room

In the quest room, in chat:

!road

The server answers with what is installed. 0 is always the live Road:

Roads: 0 live | 1 Original Road | 2 Zenith Road. Use !road <number>.

Pin one by number:

!road 1
Road set to Original Road for this room. Moving anyone still on the old Road back to town.

A number that is not on the list gets:

No such Road. Use !road to see the list.

Outside a room the command says Roads are chosen inside a quest room.

4. What the rest of the party sees

Anyone already standing in the room whose client loaded a different Road is returned to town once, with this line in their chat:

The room's Road changed. Returning you to town so your client loads it.

They walk back into the room and are then on the room's Road. Players already on that Road are not touched, and nobody standing in town or in another room is affected at all.

5. Joining a room later

The client only reads the Road file when it enters town, so a player who loaded a different Road and then walks into a pinned room is sent to town once as well:

This room is on Original Road, which your client has not loaded. Returning you to town once; re-enter the room after.

They come back in, and ready up with everyone else. The check happens on entering the room, before anyone readies, so a party cannot depart on the wrong Road by accident.

6. Going back to the live Road

!road clear
This room is back on the live Road. Moving anyone still on another Road back to town.

7. Limits worth knowing

  • A town visit loads exactly one Road, so switching between rooms on different Roads costs one town trip in between. That is how the client works, not a rule the pin adds.
  • Pins live in the game server's memory. A server restart puts every room back on the live Road.
  • Only Roads installed on the server can be chosen. A Road still being designed in the builder becomes selectable once it is deployed.
  • Clears are recorded against the Road the room ran, so an old Road's board keeps growing when people go back to it, separately from the records it earned while live.
