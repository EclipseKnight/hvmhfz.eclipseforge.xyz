Who we are

Hunsterverse is a community-run Monster Hunter Frontier Z private server, not affiliated with Capcom. This page covers this website, the account API behind it, the game server and the Discord bot.

Contact: ask a staff member on our Discord (a direct message is fine for anything private). Please don't put private requests in a report: reports go to our task board.

What we keep and why

We keep only what the server, the website and the Discord bot need to work. We don't sell it, we don't use it for ads and we don't build profiles.

Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no trackers, analytics or ads. It keeps two items in your browser's local storage, both needed for the site to work:

NameKindWhat it is forHow long
hvmhfz.tokenLocal storage, essentialKeeps you signed in after you sign inUntil you sign out, or 7 days
hvmhfz.consentLocal storage, essentialRemembers your answer to the cookie banner12 months, then we ask again

"Accept" on the banner allows optional extras; right now there are none. "Decline" changes nothing about how the site works. If we ever add something optional, it stays off until you accept.

Other services on the site

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host, GitHub sees your IP address when you load a page (GitHub's privacy statement).
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are a person on the sign-in and report forms. It runs only on those two pages and is needed for them to work; Cloudflare sees your IP address and browser details for the check (Cloudflare's privacy policy).
  • Set builder: the set builder page shows the Frontier Set Searcher by d0t-execute, a separate site also hosted on GitHub Pages. It may keep its own data in your browser.
  • Discord: links to our Discord only open Discord when you click them. If you use the Discord bot, Discord's own privacy policy applies to your messages there.

What other players can see

Character names, ranks, guilds and hunt counts appear on the public hunter, guild and leaderboard pages. Hunting Road entries show their name, description and credited character. Your user name, password, IP address, Discord user ID and @username, and which entries you voted for are never shown publicly. If your account is linked to Discord, your Discord display name is shown next to your character on the Worlds page while you are online.

To stop this, unlink your account (Discord bot /unlink) or leave our Discord.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • see the data we keep about you, and get a copy;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete your account and its characters, or a single report or Road entry;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • change your cookie choice at any time (button above).

Ask on Discord. We answer within one month. If you live in the EU or the UK and think we handled your data wrongly, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes to this page

When this page changes, the date at the top changes too. Bigger changes are announced on Discord.
