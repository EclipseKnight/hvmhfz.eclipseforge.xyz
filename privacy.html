    Who we are

    Hunsterverse is a community-run Monster Hunter Frontier Z private server, not affiliated with Capcom. This page covers this website, the account API behind it, the game server and the Discord bot.

    Contact: ask a staff member on our Discord (a direct message is fine for anything private). Please don't put private requests in a report: reports go to our task board.

    What we keep and why

    We keep only what the server, the website and the Discord bot need to work. We don't sell it, we don't use it for ads and we don't build profiles.

    Discord link: your Discord user ID, if you link your account, and your Discord @username and display name, which our API asks Discord for with that ID and refreshes about once a weekDiscord bot commands, to sign your reports with your Discord name, to show your Discord display name next to your character on the Worlds page while you are online, and so staff can find and recognise your account in the admin panel (the stored names are shown to staff only)Until you unlink it or the account is deleted (the stored names are removed within minutes of an unlink)

    Cookies and browser storage

    This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no trackers, analytics or ads. It keeps two items in your browser's local storage, both needed for the site to work, a third only if you tick a step on the Play page, and, only if your sign-in runs out while you send a report, the text you had typed in this tab's session storage:

    NameKindWhat it is forHow long
    hvmhfz.tokenLocal storage, essentialKeeps you signed in after you sign inUntil you sign out, or 7 days
    hvmhfz.report-draftSession storage, essentialKeeps what you typed in a report if your sign-in runs out, so you can sign in again and send itUntil you send the report or close the tab
    hvmhfz.playLocal storage, functionalRemembers which steps you ticked on the Play page; written only when you tick a step, never sent anywhereUntil you clear the ticks or your browser data
    hvmhfz.consentLocal storage, essentialRemembers your answer to the cookie banner12 months, then we ask again

    "Accept" on the banner allows optional extras; right now there are none. "Decline" changes nothing about how the site works. If we ever add something optional, it stays off until you accept.

    Prontier Discord bot

    Prontier is the Hunsterverse Discord bot (its terms are here). It runs on our own server and works only in the Hunsterverse Discord server, plus direct messages with people who are in it. It does not read your ordinary messages in channels, and it asks Discord for no special access to message contents or the member list. This is what it handles:

    WhatWhereHow long
    Your Discord user ID, when you run /linkOn your game account, in our game database (through the API). The bot has no database of its ownUntil you run /unlink or your account is deleted
    Your Discord user ID, display name and @username when you use a command or button (the bot gets them from Discord with every command)The ID is sent to our API to find your linked account and characters. The bot's log keeps the ID for some actions (looking up an account or character, a backup, an import, an announcement that pings players). The @username and display name appear in the replies and posts the command makes, such as /whois and the Road party postReplies and posts stay in Discord until deleted there. Log lines stay in the server's log, which rotates automatically

The bot keeps each name for 30 minutesForgotten when you leave the server (the bot tells the API), and when the API restarts
    Game password: the one you type in /passwordSent to our API, which stores it only as a one-way hash. The bot does not log or keep it, but Discord receives it as part of your command, so use a password you don't use elsewhereAs for your account
    Character backups and imports: the save files of your character (and the file you attach to /import)Built or held in the bot's memory only, never written to its disk.     Event posts: which announcements were already postedA file on the bot's server (event names and cycle numbers, nothing about people)Kept

    The bot does not store your messages, does not relay chat between Discord and the game, and does not keep a list of members or their roles. When it checks a role it asks Discord at that moment. /whois answers in the channel with a member's Discord name, game user name, user ID, Discord ID, and character names and IDs, so anyone in the channel can read it (never passwords, saves or IP addresses). Staff who run the servers can see the API's database and the bot's log. To ask us to delete or correct anything, or to unlink your account, run /unlink or message a staff member on Discord (see Your rights). Messages and files Discord holds (replies, the backup direct message) are under Discord's privacy policy; delete them in Discord.

    Crash reports and usage data from the game

    We aren't stealing your data, we just need to know what your client is doing in order to identify issues. With the new patch, the game sends crash reports and usage data to Hunsterverse, to help fix bugs in the patch, the mods and the server. It is on by default, and you can turn it off. The first time you are in town with the new patch, the game tells you once, with two buttons: OK (keep sending) and Turn off. You can change it any time in the game (Ctrl+F10, Game options, Data tab); switching it off stops sending at once and drops anything still waiting to be sent.

    What is sent:

    • a random session number, made new each time the game starts;
    • your character id (not its name);
    • the patch and mod versions, and which mods are on;
    • the frame rate;
    • where you are in the game (stage and quest id);
    • errors and known problems the game noticed;
    • after a crash, the crash report the game writes on your PC (where in the game's code it crashed, the last things that happened in the game, your Windows version, language setting and memory, and the names of the files in the game folder), without your character name.

    What is never sent: chat, passwords, your character name, your Windows user name or folders, and other players' data. We do not keep your IP address with it.

    It is kept for 30 days, and only staff can see it. Either way, the game still writes its own local files (hvdiag.txt, hvmods.log and crash files) on your PC, and you can still upload them yourself on the Account page.

    Other services on the site

    • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host, GitHub sees your IP address when you load a page (GitHub's privacy statement).
    • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are a person on the sign-in, sign-up and report forms. It runs only on the home page and the report page and is needed for them to work; Cloudflare sees your IP address and browser details for the check (Cloudflare's privacy policy).
    • Set builder: the set builder page shows the Frontier Set Searcher by d0t-execute, a separate site also hosted on GitHub Pages. It may keep its own data in your browser.
    • Discord: links to our Discord only open Discord when you click them. If you use the Discord bot, Discord's own privacy policy applies to your messages there.

    What other players can see

    Character names, ranks, guilds and hunt counts appear on the public hunter, guild and leaderboard pages. Hunting Road entries show their name, description and credited character. Your user name, password, IP address, Discord user ID and @username, and which entries you voted for are never shown publicly. If your account is linked to Discord, your Discord display name is shown next to your character on the Worlds page while you are online.

    To stop this, unlink your account (Discord bot /unlink) or leave our Discord.

    Your rights

    Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

    • see the data we keep about you, and get a copy;
    • correct it if it is wrong;
    • delete your account and its characters, or a single report or Road entry;
    • restrict or object to how we use it;
    • change your cookie choice at any time (button above).

    Ask on Discord. We answer within one month. If you live in the EU or the UK and think we handled your data wrongly, you can also complain to your data protection authority.
