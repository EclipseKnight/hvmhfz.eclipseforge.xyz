    Menus and Town NPCs

    A tour of the in-game menus, options, chat and Guild Card, and of every NPC in town, the Caravan area and your house, with what each one does.

    Launcher and game settings

    You start the game from a launcher: it logs you in and lists your characters. Most settings are changed in game, from the start menu's Options (below).

    Different. On Hunsterverse you log in with the Mezeporta launcher, not the original guide's Taiwanese launcher. Start at the Play page for the install steps, including the launcher step for Windows and Linux. Create your game account in the launcher: press REGISTER and choose a username and password (case-sensitive: always type it the same way).

    Start menu

    The start menu gives quick access to a lot of information. It is also where you set most of the options that can be changed in game. Opening an item from the Items entry gives a short menu with the actions you can take on it, and the combination entries.

    Works the same. The start menu is client content. The pictures on this page are from the original guide's client, so our English patch can word some entries differently.

    Hunter Status menus

    The Hunter Status menus hold a lot of information, including your equipment details and your Guild Card. The Guild Card shows things such as your Hunter Rank, the HRP you need for the next rank, your courses, guild information, and your Attack and Defense values.

    Personal Status
    Lets you set a number of icons for your player. They flash for anyone who is checking icons and has the same ones set.
    Guild Card
    Shows a large amount of information. See Guild Card below.
    Equipment Details
    Lets you check your town and quest sets and see details on each piece of equipment.
    Tower Card
    Shows detailed information on everything to do with the Tower (Sky Corridor). See Sky Corridor.
    SR Books
    Shows details on the SR weapons you have. You cannot change settings here, such as the moveset, but you can confirm them. See Style Rank.
    Hunter Experience
    A collection of trophies for things you have done, depending on points in various categories. Other players can view them, but they mean nothing.
    Story Progress
    Shows which step you are on in each story questline (the series at the Series Quest Counter).
    Area Info
    Shows information on the current area.
    Monster Info
    Shows information on many monsters, including maximum and minimum sizes and the number hunted. The guide says you must first buy the book on that monster type from the shop; our General Store sells them (Buy > Books (Combo, Box, Monsters), more as your rank rises).
    Ancient Treasures
    Shows which treasures you have collected in the Tower and what effects they have enabled. The picture calls this entry Tower Treasures.

    The picture also lists a Diva Defense Card, Style Rank Settings and a Transcend Card. The guide does not describe them here. See Diva Defense, Style Rank and Transcend.

    Works the same. These menus work the same. The Diva Defense Card shows your points, gem and songs since 4 Nov, when Diva Defense went live (new, not much played yet); a few of its lists, such as the daily and norma lists, are still empty.

    Community menus

    The Community menus hold most of what you need to deal with other players, including sending mail and managing the blacklist.

    The Friends List shows everyone you have registered as a friend. It works one way: other players do not need to have you on their list for you to have them. The Joinable toggle sets whether players who have you on their friends list can jump straight to your lobby.

    Works the same. The Community menus work the same. The mail list shows up to 40 mails.

    Options menus

    The pictures below are from the original game. Our client's Options Menu has nine pages: Display (Town), Display (Gen, Quests), Input Options, NPC Settings, Chat, Sound, Post Screenshot, Display (High Grade) and Other. The Display (High Grade) page holds the High Grade rendering switches.

    The NPC Settings page sets the priority of your AI companions. See AI Companions. The companion chat switches (Rasta Chat, Partner Chat, Partnya Chat, Fosta Chat, Mercenary Chat) control how much your companions talk during quests.

    Lock On modes

    You choose the lock on with the Lock On Mode option. The guide describes the Japanese lock on this way:

    Lock On Mode
    Basically a copy of the 3DS games' lock on. Type 1 locks on with a single press and resets the camera with a double press. Type 2 does the opposite. In these modes you must pause and press R1 to change targets.

    Works the same. Our client is the Japanese one with an English patch: it has the Lock On Mode setting, not the Taiwanese client's extra "TW Lock On". The setting's own help text gives the two types: TYPE 1 locks on with one press, TYPE 2 with two presses.

    Control bindings

    You change the bindings in Input Options > Key Setting, with a page for basic actions, each weapon type and system controls. The control type is chosen there too: Portable Type (button attacks, like the console games; its pad buttons are fixed), Classic Type (right-stick attacks) and keyboard and mouse types.

    The guide does not go through each weapon type and translate every action. If you want to rebind something, you will have to work out what each action is.

    Works the same. Control bindings are client settings and work the same.

    Chat

    Chat modes and gestures

    Open the in-game chat by pressing Insert (or by holding L2 and R2). There are various filters and modes.

    • Toggle filters with the buttons at the top of the chat pane.
    • Change modes with Shift+Up/Down/Left/Right.
    • To whisper to a player, press Square while in front of them, or click their name in the chat window.
    • To perform a gesture, press # and then an F key. F6 opens the second page of gestures.

    Chat commands

    CommandWhat it does
    @herePosts a link to the server you are on that other players can click.
    @reInserts the number of spots left on your quest.
    @dicePosts the result of a dice roll from 1 to 100.
    @clr1, @clr2, @clrall, @clrpastClear the chat.
    @helpShows all available commands.
    @a, @f, @p, @g, @uSend a message to one chat. In order they are Global, Local, Party, Guild and Alliance.
    <act1> to <act24>Make you perform a gesture.
    <act25> to <act48>Make you perform any legendary gestures you bought with Tower Medals (in town only).

    Works the same. These are the game's own chat commands and work the same. Hunsterverse also has server commands that start with !; type !help in game for the list.

    Town Square NPCs

    The names below are our client's own NPC names. In Mezeporta the quest counters stand in one row (General Quest Counter, G Rank Quest Counter, Series Quest Counter, Route Quest Counter), the three shops stand together (General Store, Mama Grocer, Combo Shop Master), and the Festival/Tournament Counter, Winner Quest Counter and the H.Road/Sky Corridor counter are at the far end of the counters. The Guide stands by the plaza entrance and the Clan Counter Officer on the upper level.

    Series Quest Counter
    Gives the story quests, one series at a time (Beginners and Veteran tabs), and trades some items for others (its Item exchange). The very first tutorial quests are at the rookie area's counter, "Accepting the first quest".
    Guild Master
    A town NPC (the woman with two swords in the picture). The guide has her give quests after the first tutorials, the Gu-ku quests, SR books and moveset Urgent Quests, and trade monster kills for medals that buy Talismans (shorter wait after quests, more HP at quest start, and so on; see Medal charms). Our client text has a menu with SR Menu, Transcend Menu, Trial Menu, Guuku Menu, GSR Menu and Hunting Menu that is probably hers (not checked in game). On our server quests are taken at the counters below; the Gu-ku quests, for example Gu-ku Ecology Survey Team, are in General Quest Counter > Daily Quests.
    General Quest Counter
    Gives all quests below G Rank once you are past the rookie quests: Hunter Quests, Event, Daily, Special and Clan Quests, and the Caravan Quests row.
    Caravan Quests
    In town, the Caravan quests are a row of the General Quest Counter (General Quest Counter > Caravan Quests). In the Caravan area the Caravan Quest Counter gives them (see below). The guide also has the Caravan Festival missions and their weekly rewards here; we did not check that.
    Combo Shop Master
    Does special combining, such as pots using Halk feathers, and sells combination items. The guide also has him combine in batches, exchange gallery tickets for rewards and monster kill medals for Talismans; we did not check those.
    Mama Grocer
    The food store: sells most of the bento ingredients. See Lunchboxes below.
    Guide
    By the plaza entrance. Hands out items sent to you: event items, compensation items and other gifts (its Receive Items menu). The guide also has title quests and a stamp exchange here; we did not check those.
    Festival/Tournament Counter
    You turn in Festival Points here. It has a table of all monsters and the points they are worth, shows the current team rankings, and trades festival points for items. See Hunter Festival.
    Winner Quest Counter
    Gives the Winners Quests to the team that won the Festival (Winner Quest Counter > Winners Quests). The guide says you take them with the medals you received for your festival points.
    Furniture
    The General Store's Farm & Furniture menu sells furniture and farm tools. The game's My Gallery help says furniture is bought there, with quest materials and zenny, and placed freely in My Gallery; the Decor Derby scores your layout and gives a prize in exchange for a placed piece of furniture. The guide's stamps for farm upgrades were not found in our client's text. See House.
    G Rank Quest Counter
    Gives G Rank quests, including Event Quests and the Conquests (its Conquest・Shiten Quests row).
    Clan Counter Officer
    The guild counter: create a guild, search for one and join it.
    General Store
    Sells most consumable items. Its menu: Buy, Sell, GCP Exchange, NetCafe Benefits, Daily Special, Buy to Box, Gacha Coin Store and Farm & Furniture. Buy has six tabs: Basics (Consumables), Books (Combo, Box, Monsters), Ammo (Coatings Bullets), Tools (Mining Gathering), PVP (Pet Items) and Special (Rare Items, Food). It sells honey and the books for the monster list, combining, house expansion and box expansion (the game's own shop list).
    H.Road/Sky Corridor counter
    Gives out the Hunting Road and Tower (Sky Corridor) quests and their rewards (H.Road/Sky Corridor menu).

    Lunchboxes

    The Guide's Receive Items menu has Event Items, Purchased Items, Compensation Items and Promotional Items. Here nothing is bought with real money: gifts and event prizes arrive there.

    Different. There is no subscription service here: every account has the Trial, Hunter Life, Extra, Premium and NetCafe courses, which never run out (the server always sends an expiry of 1 January 2030), so course-only options and shop stock should apply (not tested in game). Anyone can create a guild at the Clan Counter Officer. The Hunter Festival runs, and since 4 Nov so do Diva Defense and the event campaigns (the Conquest War is off again) (see Events).

    Blacksmith NPCs

    Master Blacksmith
    Does all the basic crafting and upgrading until G Rank. Still does most of it even then. You can also open the full equipment box here.
    Master Felyne
    The Smithy's cat smith. The guide has it make armour and weapons for Partnyas, randomised Gou Weapons, and Sigils at G Rank; our client says the sigil recipe tickets go to the Master Felyne, and the Apprentice Felyne is the tutorial and Challenge cat, not a smith.
    Gacha Felyne
    Lets you draw gachas (lotteries) for rewards. See the note below for what it offers here.
    Smithy's Assistant
    The gift box desk: holds items sent to you, such as daily rewards and the HRP tickets you receive for completing quests. The Smithy also has the warehouse for storing items and equipment. See Warehouse below.
    Tower Gear Felyne
    Makes and adjusts Tower Weapons. See Tower weapons.
    Legendary craftsman
    Not in the Smithy. The guide says he used to upgrade some G Rank weapons.

    Warehouse

    The original guide says you can open the warehouses 5 times every 12 hours. Hunsterverse There is no limit here: the server always lets you in and does not count how often you open them. Each time you open one, you can make unlimited transfers between both equipment and normal boxes. You have 10 pages of each, and each page can be renamed.

    When you transfer items, you see your main box and the warehouse box. To reach the warehouse box, press left or right until you move out of the active box and into the see-through one. Sending items works like moving them between your inventory and your box: select the item, then choose how many to send.

    Different. The Gacha Felyne has our own gachas since 5 Nov: a Daily Free Draw, three Materials Gachas, a Coin Step-Up, a Coin Lucky Box and an Event Ticket Box, paid with zenny, materials, free weekly coins or event tickets. Nothing is paid for with real money, and the old lottery-only gear is not in them. See Gacha. The Master Blacksmith, Tower Gear Felyne and warehouse work as described.

    Caravan Area NPCs

    These NPCs are in the Caravan area (Pallone). The names are our client's where we could match them; where we could not, the NPC is described by its job. See Caravan for how the Caravan works.

    First Caravan Gem
    Our client's help calls this NPC the Boss: "Talk to the Boss at the Caravan and get the Caravan Jewel." The guide says she gives you a level 0 gem when you first talk to her. The Raviente (Great Slaying) quests are taken at the Caravan Quest Counter (Caravan Quest Counter > Great Slaying Quests), and Kiel hands out the Raviente rewards.
    Caravan Quest Counter
    Gives the Caravan quests: ★ Rank Quests, G Rank Quests, Great Slaying Quests and Pioneer Quests.
    Combo Shop Master
    Combines items in batches. Does special combining for items made with Caravan materials. Sells various combination items.
    Store
    Sells standard store items.
    Mama Grocer
    Sells food and packs lunchboxes.
    Upper food store
    Packs lunchboxes. Specifically for packing the larger lunchboxes that use four items for the various Raviente hunts.
    Caravan Gathering Spots
    Lets you set up gathering on the various maps in exchange for CP, materials and time. Some items can only be gotten this way, such as those needed to craft a full Unknown armour set.
    CP store
    Trades CP for various Caravan items, including Whetstones that give +10% Affinity and materials for Caravan weapons.
    Gem smith
    Upgrades and maintains the Caravan Gem. Lets you roll a gem colour or get a rainbow gem. See Caravan Gems.
    Caravan leader
    The guide says he gives a 2 hour buff in exchange for 200k CP.

    Unknown, not checked. We checked that Caravan scores post at the end of a quest (fixed 2 Nov); since 4 Nov the Caravan rankings show real scores, with no weekly gifts (see the 4 Nov changelog). The Caravan Gathering Spots (Pioneering) and the Caravan's shop boxes have no server side here (see Pioneering). The CP shop and gem smith were not checked in game. Raviente runs (see Events).

    House facility NPCs

    These NPCs are in the parts of your house. The House and AI Companions page explains each facility.

    Main house

    House Cook (our client: Waiter Felyne)
    Cooks meals for you and anyone in your house, bypassing any bentos posted with quests. This is useful for eating Adrenaline food without needing everyone to lose HP. Talking to him gives you an overview of the food you have. Sit at one of the dining tables, which come in different sizes, to actually have a meal prepared.

    Gu-ku Farm

    Felyne Caretaker
    Looks after your Gu-ku. Talk to him to receive all shinies dropped by your Gu-ku in the area. Gives access to Gu-ku Cooking and creates decorative items for Gu-ku.
    Farm tools
    The guide says a cat lets you use the Gu-ku Farm tools once per quest taken. Our client has a Straw Hat Felyne and Garden Felynes here; which one it is was not checked.
    Delivery Felyne
    Lets you transfer items between characters on your account. The guide says this needs a subscription; here every account has the courses.
    Lost & Found Felyne
    A cat who wants you to find items it lost in town. It has a chance to appear after normal quests (not deliveries). It can give exclusive items, such as crowns that are used to craft armour sets.

    My Support Island

    Halk Instructor
    Manages every part of your Halk. Our client's Halk Instructor menu also has Hire Partnya (next to Check Status, Training, Name Change and Skill Book Settings).

    My Tore

    My Tore manager (Eldest, Middle or Youngest Sister)
    A girl whose looks vary with the outfits you bought for her and with which of the three sisters you chose when setting up My Tore. Gives daily points. Lets you spend points on My Tore facilities and buy the various cat stores. Gives special missions for exclusive items such as G Whetstones. Converts extra points into GCP.
    Cat store manager
    Manages the cat stores you have set up in Felyne Square. Turns wandering cats, which you can hire, on and off.
    Partnya manager
    Lets you pick which facility your Partnya works at.

    Our client names the other My Tore NPCs the Agency Felyne and the Guild Secretary; which of them does each job above was not checked.

    SR Challenge Room

    Hiden Instructor S
    Gives out various vanity challenges. Lets you take the quests that raise the final four stats on your Book of Secrets. See Style Rank.
    Hiden Instructor R
    Gives out various vanity challenges.

    Works the same. These NPCs are game content. Moving items between your characters was a subscriber option; every account has the Extra course (not tested in game), and extra character slots cost nothing here: some accounts have five characters (the server sets no limit of its own; we did not check the client's maximum).

    Guild Card

    The Guild Card has several pages. Here is each one, in the guide's order. The pictures are from the original guide's client, so their labels are not our client's; some carry the guide author's notes in English.

    Press Square (or Z on the keyboard) on your Guild Card to open a section where you can change your comment and background and see who has your Rasta.

    • Comments simply appear on your Guild Card when other people check it.
    • A monster's background is unlocked after you have killed 100 of its type and bought its related book in the shop.
    • For more on Rastas, see Rastas.
    • You can change your title at the bookshelf in your House.

    Different. The courses page shows the same courses on every account (Trial, Hunter Life, Extra, Premium and the NetCafe courses, with an expiry of 1 January 2030 that the server sends again at every login). The Conquests page belongs to the Conquest War, which is off here.
