    Menus and Town NPCs

    A tour of the in-game menus, options, chat and Guild Card, and of every NPC in town, the Caravan area and your house, with what each one does.

    Launcher and game settings

    The original guide begins this page with the Taiwanese launcher: its login window, the character list and a configuration window with graphics and sound tabs. The guide's own advice is that you only use those options once, because all other settings are changed in game. It also says to ignore the last tab, Proxy Settings, which is useless to almost everyone. The launcher and its timezone advice were for Capcom's Taiwanese servers.

    Different. On Hunsterverse you log in with the Mezeporta launcher; the Taiwanese launcher and its proxy tab do not exist here. Start at the Play page for the install steps, including the launcher step for Windows and Linux. Create your game account in the launcher: press REGISTER and choose a username and password (case-sensitive: always type it the same way).

    Start menu

    The start menu gives quick access to a lot of information. It is also where you set most of the options that can be changed in game. Opening an item from the Items entry gives a short menu with the actions you can take on it, and the combination entries.

    Works the same. The start menu is client content; menu names can differ slightly in our English patch.

    Hunter Status menus

    The Hunter Status menus hold a lot of information, including your equipment details and your Guild Card. The Guild Card shows things such as your Hunter Rank, the HRP you need for the next rank, active subscriptions, guild information, and your Attack and Defense values.

    Personal Status
    Lets you set a number of icons for your player. They flash for anyone who is checking icons and has the same ones set.
    Guild Card
    Shows a large amount of information. See Guild Card below.
    Equipment Details
    Lets you check your town and quest sets and see details on each piece of equipment.
    Tower Card
    Shows detailed information on everything to do with the Tower or Sky Corridor. See the Tower section of the guide.
    SR Books
    Shows details on the SR weapons you have. You cannot change settings here, such as the moveset, but you can confirm them. See Style Rank.
    Hunter Experience
    A collection of trophies for things you have done, depending on points in various categories. Other players can view them, but they mean nothing.
    Story Progress
    Shows which mission you are on in each story questline (the ones from the Yellow Guild Sweetheart).
    Area Info
    Shows information on the current area.
    Monster Info
    Shows information on many monsters, including maximum and minimum sizes and the number hunted. You must have bought the books on that monster type from the Shop NPC.
    Ancient Treasures
    Shows which treasures you have collected in the Tower and what effects they have enabled. The picture calls this entry Tower Treasures.

    The picture also lists a Diva Defense Card, Style Rank Settings and a Transcend Card. The guide does not describe them here. See Diva Defense, Style Rank and Transcend.

    Works the same. These menus work the same. The Diva Defense Card shows your points, gem and songs since 4 Nov, when Diva Defense went live (new, not much played yet); a few of its lists, such as the daily and norma lists, are still empty.

    Community menus

    The Community menus hold most of what you need to deal with other players, including sending mail and managing the blacklist.

    The Friends List shows everyone you have registered as a friend. It works one way: other players do not need to have you on their list for you to have them. The Joinable toggle sets whether players who have you on their friends list can jump straight to your lobby.

    Works the same. The Community menus work the same. The mail list shows up to 40 mails.

    Options menus

    The Options menu has these pages. The Rendering Options page only appears when High Grade mode is on.

    The NPC Options page sets the priority of your AI companions. See AI Companions. The Chat Options page controls how much your companions talk during quests.

    Lock On modes

    The guide says the Taiwanese client has three Lock On modes. You choose them in Control Options.

    TW Lock On
    Shows icons at the bottom of the screen. Press R2 to change which monster is locked on, and L1 to lock. It is active on the "Off" option. Because it was added before the official version, it has quirks, such as letting you lock on to burrowed or invisible monsters.
    JP Lock On
    Basically a copy of the 3DS games' lock on. Type 1 locks on with a single press and resets the camera with a double press. Type 2 does the opposite. In these modes you must pause and press R1 to change targets.

    Unknown, not checked. Our client is the Japanese one with an English patch. Which Lock On modes it offers was not checked.

    Control bindings

    You can change the bindings for every control type in the Control Bindings section of the Control Options menu. For PSP-style controls, choose the second option in the first drop-down on the controller section.

    The guide does not go through each weapon type and translate every action. If you want to rebind something, you will have to work out what each action is.

    Works the same. Control bindings are client settings and work the same.

    Chat

    Chat modes and gestures

    Open the in-game chat by pressing Insert (or by holding L2 and R2). There are various filters and modes.

    • Toggle filters with the buttons at the top of the chat pane.
    • Change modes with Shift+Up/Down/Left/Right.
    • To whisper to a player, press Square while in front of them, or click their name in the chat window.
    • To perform a gesture, press # and then an F key. F6 opens the second page of gestures.

    Chat commands

    CommandWhat it does
    @herePosts a link to the server you are on that other players can click.
    @reInserts the number of spots left on your quest.
    @dicePosts the result of a dice roll from 1 to 100.
    @clr1, @clr2, @clrall, @clrpastClear the chat.
    @helpShows all available commands.
    @a, @f, @p, @g, @uSend a message to one chat. In order they are Global, Local, Party, Guild and Alliance.
    <act1> to <act24>Make you perform a gesture.
    <act25> to <act48>Make you perform any legendary gestures you bought with Tower Medals (in town only).

    Works the same. These are the game's own chat commands and work the same. Hunsterverse also has server commands that start with !; type !help in game for the list.

    Town Square NPCs

    Story and Item Trading Guild Sweetheart
    Gives the first tutorial quests and the story quests. You can also trade for some items here.
    Guild Mistress
    Gives quests after the first tutorials, the Gook quests, SR books and moveset urgents. You can trade monster kills for medals here, to get Talismans that reduce the wait after quests, give more HP at quest start, and so on. See Medal charms.
    Main Quest Guild Sweetheart
    Gives all non-G Rank quests after you progress past the Guild Mistress.
    Caravan Sweetheart
    Gives Caravan quests outside the Caravan Area. She has the Caravan Festival missions whenever it is on, and the rewards for that week and the week after.
    Combiner
    Combines items in batches. Does special combining, such as pots using Halk feathers. Exchanges gallery tickets for rewards and cash items for luck medals and similar. Exchanges monster kill medals for Talismans. Sells various combination items.
    Lunchbox / Ingredients NPC
    Packs lunchboxes for quests and sells most of the ingredients needed to make them. See Lunchboxes below.
    Purple Guild Sweetheart
    Updates subscriptions. Gives out purchases, event items and compensation items. Has quests for titles. Exchanges subscription stamps for HL and EX tickets.
    Hunter Festival Manager
    You turn in Festival Points to her. She has a table of all monsters and the points they are worth, shows the current team rankings, and trades festival points for items. See Hunter Festival.
    Hunter Festival Winners NPC
    Gives out quests to whichever team colour won the Festival. You take these quests with medals you received for the points you contributed.
    Furniture and Gallery NPC
    Sells furniture and makes it from materials. Trades stamps (earned by making furniture) for farm upgrades. Sells farm tools. Evaluates your gallery so you can get Gallery gems and other rewards. See House.
    G Rank NPC
    Gives out G Rank quests, including Event quests and Conquests.
    Guild Manager
    Lets you create and join guilds after you finish the tutorial. Creating a guild needs a subscription, according to the guide.
    Store Sweetheart
    Sells most consumable items, including honey. Sells the books for the monster list, combining, house expansion and box expansion. The guide says her stock is much better when you are subscribed than on the trial.
    Tower NPC
    Gives out Tower quests and rewards while the Tower is available.

    Lunchboxes

    For reference, the Purple Guild Sweetheart's menu lists Manual, Title Quests, Ticket Exchange, Receive Items and Update Subs. Receive Items opens a second menu.

    That second menu has Event Items, Purchased Items, Compensation Items and Promotional Items.

    Different. There is no subscription service here: every account has the Trial, Hunter Life, Extra, Premium and NetCafe courses, which never run out (the server always sends an expiry of 1 January 2030), so subscriber-only options (such as creating a guild) and shop stock should apply (not tested in game). The Hunter Festival runs, and since 4 Nov so do Diva Defense and the event campaigns (the Conquest War is off again) (see Events).

    Blacksmith NPCs

    Blacksmith
    Does all the basic crafting and upgrading until G Rank. Still does most of it even then. You can also open the full equipment box here.
    Cat Smith
    Makes armour and weapons for Partnyaas. Makes randomised Gou Weapons. Makes Sigils at G Rank.
    Lottery Cat
    Lets you gamble with various items for rewards. According to the guide it was mostly paid for with real money, mostly at poor odds, and lotteries were time limited. That was the official service; see the note below for what applies here.
    Warehouse Manager and Warehouse NPC
    Hold the items given as daily rewards and the HRP tickets you receive for completing quests. They also have a warehouse for storing items and equipment. See Warehouse below.
    Tower Weapons Cat
    Makes and adjusts Tower Weapons. See the Tower section of the guide.
    Legendary Blacksmith
    Does not appear at the moment. Previously used to upgrade some G Rank weapons.

    Warehouse

    You can open the warehouses 5 times every 12 hours. Each time you open one, you can make unlimited transfers between both equipment and normal boxes. You have 10 pages of each, and each page can be renamed.

    When you transfer items, you see your main box and the warehouse box. To reach the warehouse box, press left or right until you move out of the active box and into the see-through one. Sending items works like moving them between your inventory and your box: select the item, then choose how many to send.

    Different. The lotteries are not available here yet: the gacha screen holds only three demo gachas with small zenny prizes, and nothing is paid for with real money. The Blacksmith, Tower Weapons Cat and warehouse work as described.

    Caravan Area NPCs

    These NPCs are in the Caravan Area. See Caravan for how the Caravan works.

    Kashira
    Gives you a level 0 gem when you first talk to her. She is also used to set up Raviente hunts.
    Caravan Sweetheart
    Gives the various Caravan quests in Pallone.
    Caravan Combiner
    Combines items in batches. Does special combining for items made with Caravan materials. Sells various combination items.
    Store NPC
    Sells standard store items.
    Food NPC
    Sells food and packs lunchboxes.
    Upper Food NPC
    Packs lunchboxes. Specifically for packing the larger lunchboxes that use four items for the various Raviente hunts.
    Caravan Gathering Spots
    Lets you set up gathering on the various maps in exchange for CP, materials and time. Some items can only be gotten this way, such as those needed to craft a full Unknown armour set.
    CP Store
    Trades CP for various Caravan items, including Whetstones that give +10% Affinity and materials for Caravan weapons.
    Caravan Gem Smith
    Upgrades and maintains the Caravan Gem. Lets you roll a gem colour or get a rainbow gem. See Caravan Gems.
    Caravan Leader
    Gives a 2 hour buff in exchange for 200k CP.

    Unknown, not checked. We checked that Caravan scores post at the end of a quest (fixed 2 Nov); since 4 Nov the Caravan rankings show real scores, with no weekly gifts (see the 4 Nov changelog; new, not much played yet). The CP shop and gem smith were not checked. Raviente runs (see Events).

    House facility NPCs

    These NPCs are in the parts of your house. The House and AI Companions page explains each facility.

    Main house

    House Cook
    Cooks meals for you and anyone in your house, bypassing any bentos posted with quests. This is useful for eating Adrenaline food without needing everyone to lose HP. Talking to him gives you an overview of the food you have. Sit at one of the dining tables, which come in different sizes, to actually have a meal prepared.

    Gook Farm

    Gook Manager
    Looks after your gooks. Talk to him to receive all shinies dropped by gooks in the area. Gives access to Gook Cooking and creates decorative items for gooks.
    Farm Tools Cat
    Lets you use the Gook Farm tools once per quest taken.
    Mail Cat
    Lets you transfer items between characters on your account. The guide says this needs a subscription.
    Lost Cat
    A cat who wants you to find items it lost in town. It has a chance to appear after normal quests (not deliveries). It can give exclusive items, such as crowns that are used to craft armour sets.

    My Support Island

    My Support Manager
    Manages every part of your Halk. Lets you hire Partnyaas.

    Pugi Farm

    Pugi Farm Manager
    A girl whose looks vary with the outfits you bought for her and with which of the three sisters you chose when setting up the Pugi Farm. Gives daily points. Lets you spend points on Pugi Farm facilities and buy the various cat stores. Gives special missions for exclusive items such as G Whetstones. Converts extra points into GCP.
    Pugi Farm Cat Manager
    Manages the cat stores you have set up in Airou Plaza. Turns wandering cats, which you can hire, on and off.
    Pugi Farm Partnyaa Manager
    Lets you pick which facility your Partnyaa works at.

    SR Challenge Room

    The S NPC
    Gives out various vanity challenges. Lets you take the quests that raise the final four stats on your Book of Secrets. See Style Rank.
    The R NPC
    Gives out various vanity challenges.

    The guide labels these two NPCs only "S" and "R".

    Works the same. These NPCs are game content. Moving items between your characters was a subscriber option; every account has the Extra course (not tested in game), and extra character slots cost nothing here: some accounts have five characters (the server sets no limit of its own; we did not check the client's maximum).

    Guild Card

    The Guild Card has several pages. Here is each one, in the guide's order. The Chinese labels on the first pictures are the Taiwanese client's own text. Some pictures carry the guide author's notes in English.

    Press Square (or Z on the keyboard) on your Guild Card to open a section where you can change your comment and background and see who has your Rasta.

    • Comments simply appear on your Guild Card when other people check it.
    • A monster's background is unlocked after you have killed 100 of its type and bought its related book in the shop.
    • For more on Rastas, see Rastas.
    • You can change your title at the bookshelf in your House.

    Different. The Subscription page shows the same courses on every account (Trial, Hunter Life, Extra, Premium and the NetCafe courses, with an expiry of 1 January 2030 that the server sends again at every login). The Conquests page belongs to the Conquest War, which is off here.
