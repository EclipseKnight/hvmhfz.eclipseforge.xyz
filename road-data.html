    Hunting Road data

    Every Hunting Road the server has run, newest first: per floor its Road points multipliers and the monsters it can draw. The open Road is the one parties climb; the solo Road is the one you climb alone. A Road shows each mode once: a mode that did not change says which Road it matches.

    Vote Build a Road Past winners Road data Leaderboard

    How to read it

    • Points: the floor's Road points multipliers (the second one only shows when it differs).
    • Table: the monster table the floor draws from, numbered as in the Road builder.
    • Chance: how often a monster is drawn on that floor, from the table's weights. A forced entry is marked "(forced)".
    • No monster (id 0): an entry of the table with no monster in it. It has a weight, so it counts in the chances; what the game does when it draws one is not known yet.
    • The last row of a mode covers every floor above it. Dates and votes are on Past winners.
