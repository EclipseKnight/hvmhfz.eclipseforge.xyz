    Custom quests

    Create a quest

    Up to five waiting for review or in testing at a time; approved quests don't count, so you can have as many as get approved. Build one in the browser from a blank quest, or upload a decompressed quest file you already have; either way it is yours to test straight away, in game under Event, and friends can join when you post it. An admin publishes or rejects it later. While in testing a quest pays its reward money but no item rewards; once approved it pays the rewards you set.

    A quest in testing expires after 14 days without activity: submitting it, starting it in game, or pressing Keep testing each give it 14 days again, and a reminder shows on this page 3 days before. An expired quest is kept, and you can resubmit it for testing again whenever you like. Drafts never expire. A published quest stays until an admin retires it.

    What is in your backup

    A backup holds your character's save files: equipment, item box and pouch, zenny, Hunter and G Rank progress, Road points and your equipped Road skills, your house layout, your Partner, Felynes, decoration sets, plates, Hunter Navi, story progress and favourite quests. Download it here, or use /backup in our Discord, which DMs you one zip per character.

    It does not hold what the server keeps for you: your warehouse, the shared item box of your account, your guild and its box, your Road skill levels, your Road and Sky Corridor records, rewards you already claimed, titles, mail and friends.

    Importing replaces your character's save files with the ones in the file. Log out first. Your server-side data stays as it is: anything you moved to your warehouse or guild box after the backup is still there. Road skill levels go back to what they were when the backup was made if the file is our own unchanged backup of that same character; any other file clears them, and you buy them again with the Road points in the save. Every import is logged.

    Taking your character to another server: our backup works on servers running the ZZ client, and its character.json is in the export format newer Erupe servers use. Ask that server's staff how they import saves.

    Client patch

    Your characters

      Pre-releases

      Choose which patch the Mezeporta launcher gives you at its next login: Stable, or one of the pre-releases above. Only the Mezeporta launcher follows this setting.

      Pre-release patches play only on the Test land, where nothing is saved. They cannot play on normal lands; switch back to Stable to play normally (if you try a normal land, you are switched back automatically).

      Before you try a pre-release

      • A pre-release patch plays only on the Test land, and nothing you do there is saved.
      • You cannot play on the normal lands with it. Switch back to Stable any time; if you try a normal land, the server switches you back for you.
      • Choosing a pre-release makes you a Playtester (the playtest feedback channel on Discord opens for you). You lose that again as soon as you are back on Stable.

      Diagnostics

      The game crashed, loaded Mezeporta instead of a map, or lost the connection? The game writes a report of what happened. Upload it here for a plain answer; staff see it too.

      1. Open the game folder: right-click the game's shortcut, Open file location (the folder with mhf.exe, mhfo-hd.dll and hvmods.dll).
      2. Find hvdiag.txt, and any hvcrash-….txt file. Crash files are named by the time of the crash (UTC): take the newest.
      3. Pick the files below, or drag them from the folder onto the box: up to 10 at a time, text files only, each up to 256 KB. They are uploaded one after another.

      No hvdiag.txt? The game ran without hvmods.dll (an old patch, or the file is missing): run the launcher to update. The files hold your character's name and id and your PC's Windows version, language and memory; no passwords, no Windows user name or folder path.

      Drop your files here, or use the button to choose them.
