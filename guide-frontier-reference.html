Reference

Odds and ends: house facilities, companions, town NPCs, items and the box, the monster roster, tools, gathering and reference numbers.

Japanese account signup, client installs, VPNs, subscriptions, MyCard top-ups and lotteries were live-server concerns. Hunsterverse has no account fee, no cash shop and no paid service, so only the in-game currencies — GCP, CP, RP, medals, Pugi points — matter here.

House facilities

Main room

The equipment box handles items, every equipment change and Pugi Cuffs. The bed tests movesets; the bookshelf holds kill counts, element and Horn notes, your title and style. Your Partner and Partnyaa live here, and HRP tickets are the cheapest way to rank them.

Gook farm

The cat on the stump pays materials after each quest, one tool type per quest; tiers come from furniture stamps. Options cover plants, junk, cooked meat, ores, insects, Mocha Pots and Bargains, the last handing out Mega Pickaxes and Max Potions.

Gooks are duck-like pets that give items and unlock cooking. Hatch one by forcing a named monster to drop a shiny on its egg quest — Sonic Bomb on a Kut-Ku, tail break on a Congalala — then run two blueprint deliveries.

Gook tools — bait, pickaxe, bug net, gloves, Lost Ball farcaster — have a fixed chance to double a gather. Lost Balls stack to three and reach the secret area with a Gook Charm, bought with 99 Gook Junk (a flat 4% gather).

Cooking is a timing minigame: confirm when the Gook lands after the big bounce. Best recipe is 10 Mysterious Shards into one Mysterious Stone; shards drop at 5% from any G Rank insect spot.

Pugi farm (My Tore)

Unlocked at HR3. Points arrive daily, one then two alternating, plus 90P from the HR5–7 bonuses; leftovers convert at 30P to 3,000 GCP. Buy a Pugi (30P), the Departure Facility trampoline (30P), then an outfit (20P). The G Cuff Licence costs 60P at GR50 and gates every G Rank cuff; from GR350 a Pugi also carries one box item to use on you.

Cuffs are fixed-value charms — a +4 Earplug cuff is always +4. Airou Plaza adds stores selling G Whetstones, Disposable Earplugs and wide-area powders.

Companions

A Halk arrives at first login: eggs teach skills, feeding raises element and status, GR150 adds a third skill, and one Halk Pot a day cuts damage. Fostas are free filler hunters, gone by HR5. Rastas are player clones at HR2; Legendary Rastas were premium.

Partners and Partnyaas

A Partner needs HR2 and the first house upgrade (10,000z); pick its weapon from the legendary in the bar. Sword and Shield or Dual Swords are reliable, ranged AI is poor, and gear given to a Partner is deleted from your own box.

Partner weapons ignore most numbers: raw is fixed by tier, element reaches 1,500 at rank 999, and only status carries over fully. Armour pays defence by class — 230 G Rank, 250 Burst, 260 G Exotic, 270 Origin, 300 Zenith.

Skills are bought with GCP, gated by Partner Rank, and the top tier wants PR999: Strong Attack +5 and Encourage +2 at 40,000. Cost and slot upgrades raise the budget to 260 and the skill count to 12, effects do not stack, and a Partner uses one item type at a time.

Partnyaas are the cats: decoration-based skills like a hunter's, in trees that step in tens, fed by Guild Hall treasure hunts and Pugi Farm booths. Their damage is modest; the reason to run one is a second break roll on every part you break. Best skills are Health +100, Attack +50, Affinity +50%, Fencing +2, the flutes and Encourage.

Items and materials

With a recipe on screen, pressing L3 (or 1) on a highlighted material gives its source: a rank band and a named monster or area. Bands read HR1–99 then SR equivalents, and anything marked HC needs hardcore mode; the flags name a gathering type, a Gook farm, the N Point store or a Conquest reward.

The box holds 41 pages of items and 20 of equipment. Pages come from store books, Hunter Rank 1–7, reaching G Rank and G Rank 1–500, and Caravan Rank milestones. The equipment box only grows from rank.

Combining has two- and three-item recipes, and a recipe can have several routes: Ancient Potion is Immunizer plus Kelbi Horn, or Catalyst plus Dragon Toadstool plus Kelbi Horn. Entries list success rate and quantity, so a 65% three-item recipe is a poor bet.

Guts Tickets stop one death; Great Guts refill you. Status tickets block poison, paralysis or sleep, but not Zenith versions. The 4,000 and 20,000 GRP tickets add their value on completion and do not stack. Carving Charms add a carve, Lucky Charms add reward rolls.

Hunt Medals buy charms: Impatience cuts post-quest waiting, Gathering raises a node's count, Health and Stamina add up to +50.

The equipment and item box

The main box is at the Blacksmith, your house, the Caravan equipment tent and the Guild Hall changing room. Smaller boxes elsewhere handle items only, with no armour changes.

Equipping runs from the top menu into Equip, then asks which set you are changing: a Quest Set is what you hunt in, a Town Set what you wear in town, a Rasta Set what your clone wears for someone else.

Gear Presets save a loadout and preview its skills; they did not save Pugi Cuffs before G10, so name presets after the cuff they need. Item Presets save your inventory, with slots 1–4 bound to L1/L2/R1/R2 or F/Ctrl/C/Shift — restocking is one button.

Caravan Gem Skills, unlocked at HR2 from the Caravan gem, grant up to three slot-free skills: flat true raw, KO multipliers, no flinching.

Two gimmicks need more than a line: Espinas only turns vulnerable when enraged and its breaks only count at low health, while Zerureusu hardens whichever part is taking the most of one damage type.

Third-party tools

MHSX2G is the armour set builder: give it skills, slots and piece counts and it searches. Piece names are Japanese and it includes unreleased armour, so set filters carefully. Sigil Hunter reads sigil skills and prints them, but is unmaintained and needs a Chinese system locale and AutoHotkey. The English Ferias/Loxxol userscript translates hitbox pages and G Rank upgrades; failing that, OCR with ABBYY FineReader, Capture2Text or Hanping.

Gathering maps

One chart per area: Jungle, Desert, Swamp, Snowy Mountains, Volcano, Great Forest, Forest and Hills, Highlands, Canyon, Tidal Island, Flower Field, White Lake, Painted Falls, Polar Sea and the three Tower maps. Each lists its gathering, mining, insect and fishing points, what they yield and their rank band.

By inclination, Jungle, Great Forest and Forest and Hills are herb, mushroom and bug country; Desert and Volcano are mining maps; Swamp mixes bugs with herbs; Snowy Mountains and Polar Sea supply ice and cold-weather plants; Tidal Island, White Lake and Painted Falls lean on fishing; the Tower maps keep small pools.

Every spot has a fixed minimum and maximum gather count, which charms shift: a 4–8 spot becomes 7–8 with a minimum charm or 4–10 with a maximum. Polar Sea is G Rank only, and the Jungle's island is the one place you can gather insects indefinitely.

Purchasing things

Everything purchasable was live-server only: MH Coins, subscriptions, Premium, NetCafe, N Points, lottery coins, Pugi outfits and restyle points. None of it exists here and none of it is needed; the gear is earned. Only purchases in GCP, CP, RP, zenny and medals remain.

Quick reference sheet

Armour upgrade paths. Standard: Base → F → FX/FY → HC → HS → G → GF → GX. Limited: Base → U → R → RX and Base → S → L → LX. Gou: Gou → Storm → Supremacy → G Supremacy → Burst → Origin. Exotic: Base → G → GF → GX. Premium: Base → F → FZ → HC → HS → GS → GP → ZP, with GP needing GR1 materials and ZP GR200. G Rank: G → GF → GX. Zenith: Z → ZF → ZY → ZX at GR200, 400, 600, 800.

Raw multipliers (divide displayed attack for true raw): SnS 1.4, Dual Swords 1.4, Great Sword 4.8, Long Sword 4.8, Hammer 5.2, Hunting Horn 5.2, Lance 2.3, Gunlance 2.3, Tonfa 1.8, Switch Axe 5.4, Magnet Spike 5.4, Bowguns and Bow 1.2.

Combination elements deal both parts; the number shown is whichever the hitzone is weak to. Light is 70% fire and 70% thunder, Blaze 70/70 fire and dragon, Tenshou 30 fire, 100 water, 70 thunder, Lightning Rod 70/70 thunder and dragon, Okiko 80/80/40 fire, ice and dragon, Black Flame 50 fire and 150 dragon, Crimson Demon the reverse, Dark 80/80 ice and dragon, Music 100/100 water and ice, Sound 100/100 water and dragon, Wind 80/80 thunder and ice, Burning Zero 125/125 ice and fire, Emperor's Roar 150 thunder and 50 dragon.

Weapon length runs very short to very long and moves reach and sweet spot together: a very short Great Sword is all sweet spot and no range.

Sword Crystals are blademaster ammunition needing their own skill at +1 to +3, with +3 the maximum; apply with R2 plus R3 or Shift plus Minus.

Bowguns and bows. Shelling is normal, spread or long; Rapid Fire and Ultra Rapid Fire are separate abilities. Heavy Bowgun adds 0.3x in the first half of critical distance, where the shot expands into a ring. Coatings multiply damage — Power 1.5, others 1.4 — and Bow Hiden, an Origin or Zenith piece and Consumption Slayer add 0.2, 0.1 and 0.2.

Skills. Ten active is the default, rising to 11 or 12 with G Rank armour. Every skill has an invisible priority, so spare points can silently push a low-priority skill such as Focus off the list. Gou, Storm, Supremacy, Burst and Origin armour grant Muscle Boost, one free level to every skill, at health thresholds down to 70% for five pieces.
