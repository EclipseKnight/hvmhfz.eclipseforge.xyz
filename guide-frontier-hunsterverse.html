    Frontier guide · Hunsterverse

    Hunsterverse vs the old servers

    The Frontier guide was written for Capcom's official servers, which closed in December 2019. This page sums up how Hunsterverse compares, then lists the "On Hunsterverse" note of every section, so you can see at a glance what works the same, what is different and what is not built here yet.

    The short version

    • The game is the same. We run the final Frontier Z client (Japanese, with an English patch). Monsters, weapons, armour, skills, sigils, Transcend, crafting and the quest files are the game's own, so the guide's advice about how the game plays still holds. Menu names can differ slightly from the guide's, which used the Taiwanese client.
    • No payments. There is no Item Mall, no points to buy and nothing paid. Anything the guide says about MyCard, PayPal, PSN or Taiwanese maintenance days does not apply.
    • Courses are free and the same for everyone (details below).
    • Gacha is not set up yet. The gacha screen holds only three demo gachas with small zenny prizes, and nobody has gacha coins yet. Hunting Road rewards will pay coins from 17 Nov 2026.
    • Active Feature: every weapon type is featured every day, not a rotating few.
    • Events run on the server's own schedule, not the Taiwanese calendar: the Hunter Festival runs back to back (prizes on Events), Raviente runs (we have not checked how often a new one starts), and MezFes is open every day. Since 4 Nov Diva Defense (3-week cycles) and event campaigns run too (the Conquest War is off: ZZ already unlocks every level without it), and from 5 Nov, 15:00 UTC the Speedrun Cup (weekly) and the VS Bracket (every 14 days, play whenever; boards on Tournaments) run too: they are new and not much played yet (4 Nov changelog). The Return World was removed.
    • Not built here yet: the Grand Fish Cup, reward song effects (not tested), guild graduation and guild Tenrouirai missions in the Sky Corridor. The gacha coin shop, the N point shop and the festival point exchange have no server stock.
    • The Hunting Road is voted on. Players build Roads and vote; the winner goes live, and a party can pick any installed Road with !road. See the Roads guide, the vote and Road data. The Road shop is stocked (1,132 items).
    • Extra characters are free: some accounts here have five. The server sets no limit of its own; we have not checked the client's maximum.
    • Our own tools replace the old fan sites the guide pointed to: Monsters, Catalog, Quests (every quest on the server, in English), the Set builder and who is online.

    Course (subscription) perks

    On the official servers you paid for courses such as Hunter Life, Extra and Premium. On Hunsterverse every account has the same courses, for free, and players cannot change them. The server sends them with an expiry of 1 January 2030 and sends it again at every login, so they never run out:

    CourseOn Hunsterverse
    Hunter Life (HL), Extra (EX), PremiumOn for every account
    Net Cafe (N course, N Boost, official cafe)On for every account. The cafe gives the normal rates (no extra HRP, zenny or carves); the daily 2-hour Boost Time and the daily cafe bonus are on.
    Assist (Legend Rasta), Hiden, Hunter SupportOff for every account

    So the perks the guide ties to Hunter Life, Extra or Premium (such as the free Rainbow gem change, Premium's extra Raviente gems and Premium recommended for the Hiden grind) should apply to everyone. We read this from the accounts and the server's code; we have not tested each perk in game.

    What the official servers sent to subscribers as items is not sent here: the server answers the game's monthly course-item check with "already claimed", so there is no gift of the five "HL Hiden" tickets at GR500, Premium outfit tickets or the like (read from the server's code, not tested in game). The gifts in the in-game gift list are the ones listed on the account page.

    Diva DefenseDifferent

    Diva Defense runs here since 4 Nov, in 3-week cycles: the current one started on Mon 2 Nov.     Hunter's Road

    SectionOn Hunsterverse
    How the Road worksDifferent

    Hunsterverse runs its own Road. Players design Roads in the Road builder and vote every two weeks on the next live Road (Road vote). Every Road keeps its own board, and a quest party picks one with !road. Rewards by place are claimed on your account page, first after round 1 closes on 17 Nov 2026. See Hunting Road on Hunsterverse and Road data.

    Stores and pointsDifferent

    The Road shop is stocked here (1,132 rows over 9 lists), and none of its rows has a purchase limit set. We have not checked whether the prices and unlock steps match the guide's.

    Surviving a long runWorks the same

    Surviving the fights works the same, because combat is client content. What the floors hold changes with each Road, so check Road data for the current one.

    Tower MedalsUnknown, not checked

    Tower Medals are game content, but whether the current Road has Bonus Stages and what the medal store sells were not checked. The New & Returning Hunter Pack of the event campaigns (once per character, see the 4 Nov changelog) holds some Tower Medals. What a Road holds changes with each vote round; see Road data.

    White FatalisUnknown, not checked

    White Fatalis is game content. Whether the current Road offers him, and how the weekly limit works, were not checked. The floors change with each Road.

    Hunter's Road skillsUnknown, not checked

    Road skills are game content. Which of them our Roads use, and their costs and ranks, were not checked.

    Tower weaponsWorks the same

    Tower weapons are client crafting and should work the same (not tested in game). Resetting tower skills is saved (fixed 2 Nov). The Sky Corridor is open from 3 Nov 2026: a cleared tower quest counts toward your guild's tower missions, the town's Duremudira progress follows everyone's clears and floors, and the tower present box gives Tower Medals and Ancient Gold Debris at set numbers of floors and clears (our own list, once per character). With the 4 Nov client patch (changelog) clears count for hunters without a guild too; with an older patch only hunters in a guild are counted, because the game reports a cleared tower quest only for them. On the Duremudira floor the crumbled wall can be reached again: walk to the broken wall to Return to Mezeporta, which counts as a clear and starts your next run from the next floor (3 Nov). See Sky Corridor for the boards. A full tower run has not been tested in game yet.

    Tower armour is client crafting and should work the same (not tested in game). The Sky Corridor, where its materials come from, is open from 3 Nov 2026 (see Tower weapons).

    DuremudiraUnknown, not checked
