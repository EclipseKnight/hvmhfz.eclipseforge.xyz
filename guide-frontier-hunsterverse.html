    Frontier guide · Hunsterverse

    Hunsterverse vs the old servers

    The Frontier guide was written for Capcom's official servers, which closed in December 2019. This page sums up how Hunsterverse compares, then lists the "On Hunsterverse" note of every section, so you can see at a glance what works the same, what is different and what is not built here yet.

    The short version

    • The game is the same. We run the final Frontier Z client (Japanese, with an English patch). Monsters, weapons, armour, skills, sigils, Transcend, crafting and the quest files are the game's own, so the guide's advice about how the game plays still holds. Menu names can differ slightly from the guide's, which used the Taiwanese client.
    • No payments. There is no Item Mall, no points to buy and nothing paid. Anything the guide says about MyCard, PayPal, PSN or Taiwanese maintenance days does not apply.
    • Courses are free and the same for everyone (details below).
    • The gacha is our own. Since 5 Nov the Gacha Felyne has a Daily Free Draw, three Materials Gachas, a Coin Step-Up, a Coin Lucky Box and an Event Ticket Box. Every character gets 10 free Trial Coin G each week, and Hunting Road prizes pay Golden Coin G. The old lottery-only gear (PZ cuffs, ZP armour, Skill Fruits) is not in them. See Gacha.
    • Active Feature: every weapon type is featured every day, not a rotating few.
    • Events run on the server's own schedule, not the Taiwanese calendar: the Hunter Festival (prizes on Events) takes turns with Diva Defense (3-week cycles, since 4 Nov), and the Speedrun Cup (weekly) takes turns with the VS Bracket (every 14 days, play whenever; boards on Tournaments) since 5 Nov. MezFes is open every day, and event campaigns and every event quest are always on. Raviente runs: register at Kieru, then type !ravi start in chat to start any siege, Berserk and Extreme included, with however many hunters you have; the server makes your damage count more in a small group (details). The Conquest War is off: ZZ already unlocks every level without it (4 Nov changelog). The Return World was removed.
    • Not built here yet: the Grand Fish Cup, reward song effects (not tested), and guild graduation. (Guild Tenrouirai missions in the Sky Corridor work here: setting, cancelling and counting a target are stored.) The Pallone Caravan's server side (its rewards, shop boxes and Pioneering) is not built either. The festival point exchange has no server stock; what the N point shop holds was not checked.
    • The Hunting Road is voted on. Players build Roads and vote; the winner goes live, and a party can pick any installed Road with !road. See the Roads guide, the vote and Road data. The Road shop is stocked (over 2,000 rows; every row is on Sources).
    • Extra characters are free: some accounts here have five. The server sets no limit of its own; we have not checked the client's maximum.
    • Every system in one place (Hunter Navi, Partner, Halk, Poogie, guild, Caravan, Diva, Raviente, Road, Tower and more), each with what differs here: Game systems.
    • Our own tools replace the old fan sites the guide pointed to: Monsters, Items, Quests (every quest on the server, in English), the Set builder and who is online.

    Course (subscription) perks

    On the official servers you paid for courses such as Hunter Life, Extra and Premium. On Hunsterverse every account has the same courses, for free, and players cannot change them. The server sends them with an expiry of 1 January 2030 and sends it again at every login, so they never run out:

    CourseOn Hunsterverse
    Hunter Life (HL), Extra (EX), PremiumOn for every account
    Net Cafe (N course, N Boost, official cafe)On for every account. The cafe gives the normal rates (no extra HRP, zenny or carves); the daily 2-hour Boost Time and the daily cafe bonus are on.
    Assist (Legend Rasta), Hiden, Hunter SupportOff for every account

    So the perks the guide ties to Hunter Life, Extra or Premium (such as the free Rainbow gem change, Premium's extra Raviente gems and Premium recommended for the Hiden grind) should apply to everyone. We read this from the accounts and the server's code; we have not tested each perk in game.

    What the official servers sent to subscribers as items is not sent here: the server answers the game's monthly course-item check with "already claimed", so there is no gift of the five "HL Hiden" tickets at GR500, Premium outfit tickets or the like (read from the server's code, not tested in game). The gifts in the in-game gift list are the ones listed on the account page.

    Basics and Getting Started

    SectionOn Hunsterverse
    Welcome to FrontierDifferent

    These systems are part of the game client, and Raviente and the Hunting Road run here; the Caravan's server side (its rewards, shop boxes and Pioneering) is not built. The Road is different from the official servers: players vote on its design (see the Hunting Road guide). Zenith quests start at GR 300 here, not GR 200 (★1 to ★4 at GR 300, 500, 700 and 900).

    Where to go next in this guideDifferent

    The Hunter Festival, Raviente and the Caravan run here, and since 4 Nov so do Diva Defense, MezFes every day and event campaigns (Conquest is off). The weekly Speedrun Cup and the VS Bracket (every 14 days) run since 5 Nov. Every system and what differs here, in one place: Game systems. What is running now is on Events, the Cup and bracket boards on Tournaments.

    Getting started: install, account and characterDifferent

    The guide's own setup chapters (Windows locale and time zone, Locale Emulator, PlayStation versions, Taiwanese and Japanese account sign-ups, installers, VPNs) were written for Capcom's official servers and do not apply. Start at the Play page (Windows, Linux / Steam Deck, or bringing a character from another server), then create your account in the Mezeporta launcher: press REGISTER and choose a username and password (case-sensitive: always type it the same way). No time zone or locale change is needed; our Windows guide says Locale Emulator is only needed if the game refuses to start.

    First login and settingsWorks the same

    The Hunter Navi tasks are client content, and Hunter Navi is switched on here (older Hunter Navi saves were fixed on 2 Nov). Settings are client options.

    The town and Hunter Rank 1Works the same

    The town, the HR1 quests and the free rewards from the guide tasks are game content and work the same.

    Hunter Rank 2Different

    You do not need to sell a big stack of cheques here: Low and High Rank armour upgrades are cheap since client patch v1.19 (one Armor Sphere a level). Keep the cheques and sell one when you need zenny. Which items our town shop sells was not checked in game; our item research found no shop that sells the Powercharm, the Armorcharm or the Storage Guide books, and your box grows with your rank anyway (the "More Storage!" gift at the town entrance NPC also adds box pages). The Caravan Gem and its skills are game content, but the Caravan's server side (its rewards, shop boxes and Pioneering) is not built here (see Caravan). We did not test creating a Partner at the Rasta bar. Restyle points were bought with real money on the official servers: nothing is sold here, but our gift list holds some "ReStyle your Chara!" items (not tested). The path we advise is on Progression.

    Hunter Rank 3 and 4Different

    Transcend, bentos and My Tore are game content. Joining the newbie guild always fails here: join a player guild, whose Guild Store Cat sells the bento foods from guild rank 4. Our item research found no seller for the Storage Guide books (the box expansion), so you may not be able to buy it here (not checked in game); your box still grows with every rank. The 20 extra preset slots were a subscriber perk; every account here has the Hunter Life and Extra courses, so they should be there (not tested in game). Where the guide says "midnight Taiwan time", timers the server handles count from midnight Japan time instead; we did not check each one.

    Hunter Rank 5: where the game startsDifferent

    Most of this is game content, but the GCP shop is stocked by the server. Ours holds 135 entries, all Diva Defense items (Prayer materials and Prayer Stones, GX pieces, D tickets, a few gems), so it does not sell what this guide has you buy with GCP: the Lao-Shan Claws, the Secret Proofs, the wyvern and Toa Tesukatora materials and the like. Get the Lao-Shan Claw from Lao-Shan Lung carves or the other sources on its item page; Secret Proofs come from quest rewards, and the other materials have item pages too. The G decorations above are made at the Blacksmith (Decorations > Create Decorations), but the "G Deco Quests" that give their jewels are not offered here. Since client patch v1.19 (9 Nov) each upgrade level of Low and High Rank armour costs one Armor Sphere of the right tier instead of monster parts (HC and some sets also need a Secret Proof at a few levels). The 300 GCP from the HR5 tickets was not checked in game. Every account has the Hunter Life course, so the "must be subscribed at HR5" rule does not bite.

    Hunter Rank 6 and 7: the road to G RankWorks the same

    The quests, monsters and the road to G Rank are game content. The Conquest War is off here (ZZ already unlocks every level without it; see Events), and we did not check the free bundle of conquest materials from the entrance NPC.

    The newbie server and subscriptionsDifferent

    Hunsterverse has a Newbie world (see Worlds); what it offers beyond the normal worlds was not checked. Every new hunter can take the New & Returning Hunter Pack once (potions, Mega Demondrugs and Armorskins, tickets and Tower Medals) and the Weekly Supply Drop every Monday, on the Event Campaigns screen in town (see Campaigns). Every account already has the Trial, Hunter Life and Extra courses, which never run out (the server always sends an expiry of 1 January 2030), and nothing is bought here, so there is nothing to subscribe to or pay for.

    Controls, menus and chatDifferent

    The official servers used @ commands. On Hunsterverse, server commands start with !: type !help to list them. Ones enabled include !ravi, !reload, !kqf, !psn, !timer, !playtime and !road (see Hunting Road on our server).

    Daily routineWorks the same

    The login boost is on (the five boosts last 2, 4, 2, 3 and 4 hours, as in the table), the daily quest allowance is 10 and the bonus quest allowance 3. Timers the server handles count from midnight Japan time, not Taiwan time; we did not check each daily reset. The subscription the guide asks for (full NP, Daily Quests) is there on every account (not tested in game).

    Subscriptions, courses and paid itemsDifferent

    Nothing is bought here: there is no Item Mall or paid points. Every account has the Trial, Hunter Life, Extra, Premium and NetCafe courses, which never run out (the server always sends an expiry of 1 January 2030), so their perks should apply (not tested in game), except the NetCafe rank-point multipliers, which are normal here (the cafe gives a 2-hour daily boost time instead). No account has the Assist, Hiden or Hunter Support courses, and course bonus items are not mailed out. The gacha has our own prize lists since 5 Nov (a Daily Free Draw, Materials Gachas, two coin gachas and an Event Ticket Box), paid with free weekly coins, materials or zenny: see Gacha. Selling items for Frontier Points works.

    The ZZ update: Magnet Spike, Zenith Sigils, GR999 and moreDifferent

    The ZZ content is in the final Z client we run. Our Zenith gates are 100 higher than the guide's: ★1 at GR 300, ★2 at 500, ★3 at 700 and ★4 at 900 (since 17 Nov), so read "GR200" here as GR 300 and so on. The PZ cuff lottery is not here: our gachas (since 5 Nov) do not hold PZ cuffs (see Gacha).

    English Guild and Guild Hall

    SectionOn Hunsterverse
    Guild chat firstDifferent

    The "English Guild" of the original guide was its author's own guild on the official servers. Here, browse our guilds on the guilds page (there is no official English guild); our Discord is a place to find players and parties. The chat colours and keys above were not checked in game.

    Joining a guildWorks the same

    Guilds work (over 500 exist; see Guilds). Member limits depend on guild rank: 30 members up to rank 2, 40 up to rank 6, 50 up to rank 9 and 60 from rank 10. Rookie guilds are not built here: joining one always fails. Leaders and sub-leaders can also accept applications on the Account page.

    Guild requirements and meeting placeDifferent

    These were the house rules of one guild, not game rules: each guild here sets its own, within the size limit for its rank (30 to 60 members). The Hunter Festival does run; its prize table is on Events.

    The Guild HallWorks the same

    The Hall, its quest counter and the Clan Quests are game content: 75 Quest Orders and 10 Training Support Quests are offered here. We did not test each guild quest.

    Clan PoogiesDifferent

    Clan Poogies are game content. With our client patch (v1.21 and later) the first feed always gives the buff (on the old servers it often took many crackers), and the buff lasts through a crash, disconnect or relog for 12 hours after you take it (10 Nov), so you do not need to feed again at every login. Every account has the Premium course, so the Luck Poogie stacking should apply (not tested in game). Which Poogies and outfits your guild has was not checked in game.

    Guild Legendary RastaUnknown, not checked

    We did not check the daily Guild Legendary Rasta in game. Our server answers the game's daily Legendary Rasta request with placeholder data, so it probably does not work. Rasta borrowing and contract problems were fixed on 2 Nov, and no account has the Assist course (the Legend Rasta course).

    Guild Special CookingDifferent

    Cooking works as the guide says, but a meal can be eaten for 7 days after it is cooked here (since 3 Nov), not one hour. With client patch v1.21 or later, the meal's buff lasts its 90 minutes through a crash or relog. Diva Defense comes round in turn with the Hunter Festival (dates on Events); its guild progress rewards include several of the ingredients above. The Hunting Road shop sells some others. The guild store's cooking stock and ticket prices were not checked in game.

    Felyne Treasure HuntersDifferent

    Guild treasure hunts work, but sending cats is always free here, also the same day, so the RP cost for tired cats does not apply. Hunts are kept for 7 days. Souvenirs are off here: the reward basket never lists any. The hunt length and the six areas were not checked in game.

    Felyne Adventure BoatDifferent

    The boat works as in the game: it is out for 6 hours, the game itself rolls the rewards and takes the RP cost, and each member collects once. RP here is 1 for every 15 minutes logged in (it was 1 per 30 minutes without the Net Cafe course), added when you log out, up to 65,535 (see Clan Points). Its rewards, odds and RP cost, and the Diva Defense destinations, were not checked in game.

    Armour, Decorations and Skills

    SectionOn Hunsterverse
    Making armourDifferent

    Crafting is the game's own, with one change: since client patch v1.19 (9 Nov) each upgrade level of Low and High Rank armour costs one Armor Sphere of the right tier instead of monster parts (HC and some sets also need a Secret Proof at a few levels), and making the next piece needs about a third fewer materials. G Rank, GS/GP, Zenith, FZ and SP armour are unchanged. Spheres come from mining, quests and the Hunting Road shop (10 to 90 RdP), not the GCP, N Point or Festival shops. Premium kit and lottery armour came from the Item Mall and the gacha. Here the Road shop's Limited tab sells about 270 crafting tickets for Premium, kit, lottery and collaboration gear (for example Lien, Rage, Gauss, Cielo and 2011 Kit 1 to 3), 3,000 or 10,000 RdP each, from GR 1 (not tested in game).

    Armour typesDifferent

    Armour stats and effects work the same, but Premium armour (kits, lotteries) came from the Item Mall and the gacha. There is no Item Mall here and our gachas give no armour; the Road shop sells tickets for many Premium and lottery pieces instead (see the note under Making armour). Diva Defense armour needs that event, which runs since 4 Nov; the Diva GCP item shop sells its materials (Katante, Ricante, Melente, Utante, Furante and Utaei Mat, 200 GCP each; crafting them was not tested in game). The guide gives GR500 for upgrading Exotic armour because Exotic monsters are GR500 in G Rank; on our server the G Exotic Quests open at GR 100 (see G Rank milestones), so do not plan around GR 500. In our client data the smithy offers the Exotic G upgrade from HR 100, not GR 500 (craft rank of Zinogre G Helm); the G materials come from the G Exotic quests. Our Zenith tiers are GR300, GR500, GR700 and GR900 for ★1 to ★4, not the GR200, GR400, GR600 and GR800 above.

Week 2 is the guild week and week 3 has the reward lists and the Diva coin shop. Gems, points, songs, guild progress, rewards and coins are stored. The screens and gem names are in English. It is new and not much played yet: the guild map is a simple row of cells, the reward lists are our own (thresholds scaled down from the original), and the reward songs' effect and the GCP skills were not tested in game. The Diva quests are listed on the Diva Defense screen (Int. Quests, Split Route Quests and G Rank Int. Quests; see Guild Interception below). See Events and the events changelog entry.

    Prayer Weapons and Guild InterceptionUnknown, not checked

    Diva Defense runs here (see Diva Defense) and its week 2 is the guild week. The Interception quests are in the client and listed on the Diva Defense screen (above). We did not check Guild Interception itself or the Prayer Weapons: whether their rewards, Notes and transmog tickets can be earned is not known yet. What we do have is the Diva Defense coin exchange (12 items for Diva coins: Guild Ticket, 30k Cheque, Elder Dragon Fang, Elder Dragon Jewel, Ancient Drgn Bone, Cat Eye Ore [Red] and [Blue], the Golden, Silver and Bronze Pawstamp, Pallone Trsr and Legendary Rasta Tkt), which is not the guide's Note and Ticket lists. New, not much played yet.

    CaravanDifferent

    Caravan quests are client content. Caravan scores post at quest end here, and the Caravan rankings show real scores (weekly boards, always on, no weekly gifts); see the events changelog entry. The server side of the Pallone Caravan is not built: the rewards it sends, the Caravan shop boxes and Pioneering gathering have no source here (from our item source research). The CP shop and the gem smith were not checked in game.

    Caravan GemsDifferent

    Every account has the Premium course, so the 3x CP and the free Rainbow Gem should apply (not tested in game). The gem smith and CP shop were not checked. The daily colour roll at 12:00 is Taiwan time in the guide; our server clock is Japan time.

    Caravan skillsUnknown, not checked

    Caravan skills are client data, but gem upgrades, maintenance and the CP shop were not checked here. Coins are not sold here, but every account gets gacha coins (Trial Coin G every week; see Currencies); whether the +100 PP option takes them was not checked.

    Pioneering SystemNot available here yet

    The Pioneering System is not built on our server: the server sends no Pioneering gathering, so the Pioneer Map's items have no source here (from our item source research; not tested in game).

    RavienteDifferent

    Raviente runs here, and the !ravi command is on. Player limits are 8 for regular and violent, 32 for Berserk and Extreme, and 8 for the small Berserk. These limits hold, the phase vote and support take one player at a time, and a returning player starts fresh. The game itself starts a siege only when enough hunters hold it: 4 for regular, violent and the small Berserk, 24 for Berserk and Extreme. To start one here, register at Kieru, then type !ravi start in chat (any player can). The siege starts at once, however many hunters hold it; damage done before the start does not count. With few hunters the server multiplies your damage: for Berserk and Extreme by 24 divided by the registered hunters online in your land (×24 alone, ×6 for four), for the others by 4 divided by them (×4 alone), so a small group can finish it. How parties are organised and the reward odds were not checked.

    Berserk Raviente (G Rank)Different

    Berserk Raviente has room for 32 players here (8 for the small Berserk). The game would wait for 24 hunters, more than our server has online, so start it yourself: register at Kieru, then type !ravi start in chat. Alone, your damage counts 24 times. The fight and gear are client content and work the same. On the Test land only (pre-release patch, nothing saved), any siege can also be started alone at the counter. The reward odds were not checked.

    Raviente weapons and armourWorks the same

    Raviente weapons, armour and their upgrades are client crafting and work the same. How easily the materials drop depends on Raviente runs, whose reward odds were not checked.

    Extreme RavienteDifferent

    Extreme Raviente has room for 32 players. Like Berserk, the game would wait for 24 hunters, so start it with !ravi start in chat after you register at Kieru; it needs G Rank (GR 1). It is much tougher than Berserk, so bring a party. The fight changes and the gear are client content and work the same. On the Test land only, it can be started alone at the counter and needs GR 900. The reward odds were not checked.

    Hunter's Road

    SectionOn Hunsterverse
    How the Road worksDifferent

    Hunsterverse runs its own Road. Players design Roads in the Road builder and vote every two weeks on the next live Road (Road vote). Every Road keeps its own board, and a quest party picks one with !road (the solo Road with !road solo). Each Road pays prizes by place for its design vote and its floor board, claimed on your account page. See Hunting Road on Hunsterverse and Road data.

    Stores and pointsDifferent

    The Road shop is stocked here: nine tabs with over 2,000 rows (every row is on Sources, under the Hunting Road shop). Prices and gates are our own: most rows open by GR (GR 1, GR 300 for Zenith materials, GR 800 for the Upper Shiten Key and Lock, GR 50, 150 and 350 for the Basic, G and top Hiden cuffs, GR 500 for Hiden and True Hiden decorations, GR 900 for Extreme Raviente GX decorations, GR 999 for Hiden Stamps), and a few rows show a purchase limit. The server does not count past purchases, so that limit starts again at every visit and the guide's weekly limits do not apply here. Some examples: Armor Spheres 10 to 90 RdP, Hiden decorations 25,000, True Hiden 50,000, top Hiden cuffs 35,000 (Basic 10,000, G 20,000), Raviente GX decorations 20,000 to 40,000 (Extreme Raviente GX 60,000), G Raviente materials 200, PA Cuff Tkt G 500. The shop also sells bundles of 10 Courage, Glittering and Divine gems at the same price per gem, and the Road Respec, SP Book and Cost Book (10 RdP each, Super Special tab). Rows still unlock by Road floor, checked by the game when you buy: the GR1+ Items tab at floors 11, 20, 30 and 40 (two rows at 80), the Weekly Limited tab at 10, 20 and 50, the Super Special tab at 20, 50, 60, 80 and 100, so the top rows need 100 floors, not 50 (from our shop data; not tested in game).

    Surviving a long runWorks the same

    Surviving the fights works the same, because combat is client content. What the floors hold changes with each Road, so check Road data for the current one.

    Tower MedalsDifferent

    Tower Medals are game content. Here they also come from the Sky Corridor: 5 a day from Daily Rewards and up to six daily tasks, at the H.Road/Sky Corridor counter (Tower menu), and a one-time present box; and from the event campaigns and the gachas. See Tower Medal. Whether the current Road has Bonus Stages was not checked; what a Road holds changes with each vote round (Road data).

    White FatalisDifferent

    The fight is game content. The three kills a week, the 19,800 P and the weekly limit do not apply here. The Hunting Road shop sells the ten White Fatalis materials (GR1+ Items tab, 500 RdP each) to anyone who has killed White Fatalis on the Road (not tested in game), with no weekly limit, and the White Fatalis GX decorations for 30,000 RdP each (Super Special tab, from GR 1). Whether the current Road offers him was not checked; the floors change with each Road.

    Hunter's Road skillsDifferent

    Road skills are game content and the menu is the same, but our patch changes some Costs (see above) and brings back the 13 old Tower skills. The names are the client's (the guide used other names: Health Recovery, Hunting Road Points Up, Bonus Stages Up, Resurrection Knowledge, Advancement Knowledge, Virus Protection, Frost Protection, Gatekeeper Offensive and Defensive). Which skills help on our player-designed Roads was not checked.

    Tower weaponsDifferent

    Tower weapons are client crafting and should work the same, but the Hunting Road shop sells the Courage, Glittering and Divine Gems and the Tower books, not finished weapons (from our shop data); plan one with our Tower weapons page. Resetting tower skills is saved (fixed 2 Nov). The Sky Corridor is open since 3 Nov 2026, with our own 3rd and 4th Districts (Zenith-strength bosses). Clears count for every hunter, with or without a guild; a cleared tower quest also counts toward your guild's tower missions, the town's Duremudira progress follows everyone's clears and floors, and floors, clears and Duremudira kills go on the boards. The tower present box gives Tower Medals and Ancient Gold Shards at set numbers of floors and clears (our own list, once per character). On the Duremudira floor, walk to the broken wall to Return to Mezeporta, which counts as a clear and starts your next run from the next floor (3 Nov). See Sky Corridor for the boards and Game systems for what is Test land only.

    Tower armourWorks the same

    Tower armour is client crafting and should work the same (not tested in game). The Sky Corridor, where its materials come from, is open from 3 Nov 2026 (see Tower weapons).

    DuremudiraUnknown, not checked

    Duremudira is game content, but whether the current Road triggers his urgent quests was not checked. What a Road holds changes with each vote round.

    Road as a Hiden alternativeDifferent

    You do not need this route here: the Road shop sells the finished Hiden decorations (25,000 RdP each) and the True ones (50,000 RdP), both from GR 500 (see Hiden decorations). Our Road is player-designed and changes with each vote, so the points per run differ from the guide's 100,000 for 50 floors, and our carve prices differ from the table (every row is on Sources).

    Raviente materialsDifferent

    The Road shop sells the Raviente, Violent Raviente and Berserk Raviente GX decorations for 20,000, 30,000 and 40,000 RdP each with no limit, the G Raviente materials for 200 RdP (a GX armour piece needs about 150 to 175, so 30,000 to 35,000 RdP, about one siege's worth of party Road), the Raviente Ruby and Sapphire for 1,000 and Divine Power Gems, from GR 1, and the Extreme Raviente GX decorations for 60,000 RdP each from GR 900, so the weekly-limit tables above do not apply here (from our shop data; not tested in game). Its full stock is on Sources.
