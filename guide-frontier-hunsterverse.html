    Frontier guide · Hunsterverse

    Hunsterverse vs the old servers

    The Frontier guide was written for Capcom's official servers, which closed in December 2019. This page sums up how Hunsterverse compares, then lists the "On Hunsterverse" note of every section, so you can see at a glance what works the same, what is different and what is not built here yet.

    The short version

    • The game is the same. We run the final Frontier Z client (Japanese, with an English patch). Monsters, weapons, armour, skills, sigils, Transcend, crafting and the quest files are the game's own, so the guide's advice about how the game plays still holds. Menu names can differ slightly from the guide's, which used the Taiwanese client.
    • No payments. There is no Item Mall, no points to buy and nothing paid. Anything the guide says about MyCard, PayPal, PSN or Taiwanese maintenance days does not apply.
    • Courses are free and the same for everyone (details below).
    • The gacha is our own. Since 5 Nov the Gacha Felyne has a Daily Free Draw, three Materials Gachas, a Coin Step-Up, a Coin Lucky Box and an Event Ticket Box. Every character gets 10 free Trial Coin G each week, and Hunting Road prizes pay Golden Coin G. The old lottery-only gear (PZ cuffs, ZP armour, Skill Fruits) is not in them. See Gacha.
    • Active Feature: every weapon type is featured every day, not a rotating few.
    • Events run on the server's own schedule, not the Taiwanese calendar: the Hunter Festival (prizes on Events) takes turns with Diva Defense (3-week cycles, since 4 Nov), and the Speedrun Cup (weekly) takes turns with the VS Bracket (every 14 days, play whenever; boards on Tournaments) since 5 Nov. MezFes is open every day, and event campaigns and every event quest are always on. Raviente runs: register at Kieru, then type !ravi start in chat to start any siege, Berserk and Extreme included, with however many hunters you have; the server makes your damage count more in a small group (details). The Conquest War is off: ZZ already unlocks every level without it (4 Nov changelog). The Return World was removed.
    • Not built here yet: the Grand Fish Cup, reward song effects (not tested), and guild graduation. (Guild Tenrouirai missions in the Sky Corridor work here: setting, cancelling and counting a target are stored.) The Pallone Caravan's server side (its rewards, shop boxes and Pioneering) is not built either. The festival point exchange has no server stock; what the N point shop holds was not checked.
    • The Hunting Road is voted on. Players build Roads and vote; the winner goes live, and a party can pick any installed Road with !road. See the Roads guide, the vote and Road data. The Road shop is stocked (over 2,000 rows; every row is on Sources).
    • Extra characters are free: some accounts here have five. The server sets no limit of its own; we have not checked the client's maximum.
    • Every system in one place (Hunter Navi, Partner, Halk, Poogie, guild, Caravan, Diva, Raviente, Road, Tower and more), each with what differs here: Game systems.
    • Our own tools replace the old fan sites the guide pointed to: Monsters, Items, Quests (every quest on the server, in English), the Set builder and who is online.

    Course (subscription) perks

    On the official servers you paid for courses such as Hunter Life, Extra and Premium. On Hunsterverse every account has the same courses, for free, and players cannot change them. The server sends them with an expiry of 1 January 2030 and sends it again at every login, so they never run out:

    CourseOn Hunsterverse
    Hunter Life (HL), Extra (EX), PremiumOn for every account
    Net Cafe (N course, N Boost, official cafe)On for every account. The cafe gives the normal rates (no extra HRP, zenny or carves); the daily 2-hour Boost Time and the daily cafe bonus are on.
    Assist (Legend Rasta), Hiden, Hunter SupportOff for every account

    So the perks the guide ties to Hunter Life, Extra or Premium (such as the free Rainbow gem change, Premium's extra Raviente gems and Premium recommended for the Hiden grind) should apply to everyone. We read this from the accounts and the server's code; we have not tested each perk in game.

    What the official servers sent to subscribers as items is not sent here: the server answers the game's monthly course-item check with "already claimed", so there is no gift of the five "HL Hiden" tickets at GR500, Premium outfit tickets or the like (read from the server's code, not tested in game). The gifts in the in-game gift list are the ones listed on the account page.

    Basics and Getting Started

    SectionOn Hunsterverse
    Welcome to FrontierDifferent

    These systems are part of the game client, and Raviente and the Hunting Road run here; the Caravan's server side (its rewards, shop boxes and Pioneering) is not built. The Road is different from the official servers: players vote on its design (see the Hunting Road guide). Zenith quests start at GR 300 here, not GR 200 (★1 to ★4 at GR 300, 500, 700 and 900).

    Where to go next in this guideDifferent

    The Hunter Festival, Raviente and the Caravan run here, and since 4 Nov so do Diva Defense, MezFes every day and event campaigns (Conquest is off). The weekly Speedrun Cup and the VS Bracket (every 14 days) run since 5 Nov. Every system and what differs here, in one place: Game systems. What is running now is on Events, the Cup and bracket boards on Tournaments.

    Getting started: install, account and characterDifferent

    The guide's own setup chapters (Windows locale and time zone, Locale Emulator, PlayStation versions, Taiwanese and Japanese account sign-ups, installers, VPNs) were written for Capcom's official servers and do not apply. Start at the Play page (Windows, Linux / Steam Deck, or bringing a character from another server), then create your account in the Mezeporta launcher: press REGISTER and choose a username and password (case-sensitive: always type it the same way). No time zone or locale change is needed; our Windows guide says Locale Emulator is only needed if the game refuses to start.

    First login and settingsWorks the same

    The Hunter Guide tasks are client content, and Hunter Navi is switched on here (older Hunter Navi saves were fixed on 2 Nov). Settings are client options.

    The town and Hunter Rank 1Works the same

    The town, the HR1 quests and the free rewards from the guide tasks are game content and work the same.

    Hunter Rank 2Different

    You do not need to sell a big stack of cheques here: the Powercharm and Armorcharm cost 60,000z together, and Low and High Rank armour upgrades are cheap since client patch v1.19 (one Armor Sphere a level). Keep the cheques and sell one when you need zenny. Which items our town shop sells was not checked in game; our item research found no seller for the Storage Guide books, and your box grows with your rank anyway. The Caravan Gem and its skills are game content, but the Caravan's server side (its rewards, shop boxes and Pioneering) is not built here (see Caravan). We did not test creating a Partner at the Rasta bar. Restyle points were bought with real money on the official servers: nothing is sold here, but our gift list holds some "ReStyle your Chara!" items (not tested). The path we advise is on Progression.

    Hunter Rank 3 and 4Works the same

    Transcend, bentos and the Pugi Farm are game content. Our item research found no seller for the Storage Guide books (the box expansion), so you may not be able to buy it here (not checked in game); your box still grows with every rank. The 20 extra preset slots were a subscriber perk; every account here has the Hunter Life and Extra courses, so they should be there (not tested in game). Where the guide says "midnight Taiwan time", timers the server handles count from midnight Japan time instead; we did not check each one.

    Hunter Rank 5: where the game startsDifferent

    Most of this is game content, but the GCP shop is stocked by the server. Ours holds 135 entries, all Diva Defense items (Prayer materials and Prayer Stones, GX pieces, D tickets, a few gems), so it does not sell what this guide has you buy with GCP: the Lao Shan Claws, the Secret Proofs, the wyvern and Toa Tesukatora materials and the like. Get the Lao Shan Claw from Lao-Shan Lung carves or the other sources on its item page; Secret Proofs come from quest rewards, and the other materials have item pages too. Since client patch v1.19 (9 Nov) each upgrade level of Low and High Rank armour costs one Armor Sphere of the right tier instead of monster parts (HC and some sets also need a Secret Proof at a few levels). The 300 GCP from the HR5 tickets was not checked in game. We did not check the HR5 booster pack or whether the normal store sells the G decorations as the guide says. Every account has the Hunter Life course, so the "must be subscribed at HR5" rule does not bite.

    Hunter Rank 6 and 7: the road to G RankWorks the same

    The quests, monsters and the road to G Rank are game content. The Conquest War is off here (ZZ already unlocks every level without it; see Events), and we did not check the free bundle of conquest materials from the entrance NPC.

    The newbie server and subscriptionsDifferent

    Hunsterverse has a Newbie world (see Worlds); what it offers beyond the normal worlds was not checked. Every new hunter can take the New & Returning Hunter Pack once (potions, Mega Demondrugs and Armorskins, tickets and Tower Medals) and the Weekly Supply Drop every Monday, on the Event Campaigns screen in town (see Campaigns). Every account already has the Trial, Hunter Life and Extra courses, which never run out (the server always sends an expiry of 1 January 2030), and nothing is bought here, so there is nothing to subscribe to or pay for.

    Controls, menus and chatDifferent

    The list below is the guide's list for the official game, which used @ commands. On Hunsterverse, server commands start with !: type !help to list them. Ones enabled include !ravi, !reload, !kqf, !psn, !timer, !playtime and !road (see Hunting Road on our server).

    Daily routineWorks the same

    The login boost is on, the daily quest allowance is 10 and the bonus quest allowance 3. Timers the server handles count from midnight Japan time, not Taiwan time; we did not check each daily reset. The subscription the guide asks for (full NP, Daily Quests) is there on every account (not tested in game).

    Subscriptions, courses and paid itemsDifferent

    Nothing is bought here: there is no Item Mall or paid points. Every account has the Trial, Hunter Life, Extra, Premium and NetCafe courses, which never run out (the server always sends an expiry of 1 January 2030), so their perks should apply (not tested in game), except the NetCafe rank-point multipliers, which are normal here (the cafe gives a 2-hour daily boost time instead). No account has the Assist, Hiden or Hunter Support courses, and course bonus items are not mailed out. The gacha has our own prize lists since 5 Nov (a Daily Free Draw, Materials Gachas, two coin gachas and an Event Ticket Box), paid with free weekly coins, materials or zenny: see Gacha. Selling items for Frontier Points works.

    Japanese-server tipsDifferent

    The Gacha Cat lotteries (Raviente and True Hiden decorations) are not available here yet, and the Road's floors depend on the Road players voted in (see the Hunting Road guide). Every working event quest (390) is always listed, see Events; we did not check that the Silver Rathalos quest or the Road decoration prices match the guide.

    The ZZ update: Magnet Spike, Zenith Sigils, GR999 and moreDifferent

    The ZZ content is in the final Z client we run. Our Zenith gates are 100 higher than the guide's: ★1 at GR 300, ★2 at 500, ★3 at 700 and ★4 at 900 (since 17 Nov), so read "GR200" here as GR 300 and so on. The PZ cuff lottery is not here: our gachas (since 5 Nov) do not hold PZ cuffs (see Gacha).

    Surviving the fights works the same, because combat is client content. What the floors hold changes with each Road, so check Road data for the current one.

    Tower MedalsDifferent

    Tower Medals are game content. Here they also come from the Sky Corridor: 5 a day from Daily Rewards and up to six daily tasks, at the H.Road/Sky Corridor counter (Tower menu), and a one-time present box; and from the event campaigns and the gachas. See Tower Medal. Whether the current Road has Bonus Stages was not checked; what a Road holds changes with each vote round (Road data).

    White FatalisDifferent

    The fight is game content. The three kills a week, the 19,800 P and the weekly limit do not apply here. The Hunting Road shop sells the ten White Fatalis materials (G materials tab, 500 RdP each) to anyone who has killed White Fatalis on the Road (not tested in game), with no weekly limit, and the White Fatalis GX decorations for 30,000 RdP each (Special tab, from GR 1). Whether the current Road offers him was not checked; the floors change with each Road.

    Hunter's Road skillsUnknown, not checked

    Road skills are game content. Which of them our Roads use, and their costs and ranks, were not checked.

    Tower weaponsDifferent

    Tower weapons are client crafting and should work the same, but the Hunting Road shop sells the Courage, Glittering and Divine Gems and the Tower books, not finished weapons (from our shop data); plan one with our Tower weapons page. Resetting tower skills is saved (fixed 2 Nov). The Sky Corridor is open since 3 Nov 2026, with our own 3rd and 4th Districts (Zenith-strength bosses). Clears count for every hunter, with or without a guild; a cleared tower quest also counts toward your guild's tower missions, the town's Duremudira progress follows everyone's clears and floors, and floors, clears and Duremudira kills go on the boards. The tower present box gives Tower Medals and Ancient Gold Debris at set numbers of floors and clears (our own list, once per character). On the Duremudira floor, walk to the broken wall to Return to Mezeporta, which counts as a clear and starts your next run from the next floor (3 Nov). See Sky Corridor for the boards and Game systems for what is Test land only.

    Tower armourWorks the same

    Tower armour is client crafting and should work the same (not tested in game). The Sky Corridor, where its materials come from, is open from 3 Nov 2026 (see Tower weapons).

    DuremudiraUnknown, not checked

    Duremudira is game content, but whether the current Road triggers his urgent quests was not checked. What a Road holds changes with each vote round.

    Road as a Hiden alternativeDifferent

    You do not need this route here: the Road shop sells the finished Hiden decorations (25,000 RdP each) and the True ones (50,000 RdP), both from GR 500 (see Hiden decorations). Our Road is player-designed and changes with each vote, so the points per run differ from the guide's 100,000 for 50 floors, and our carve prices differ from the table (every row is on Sources).

    Raviente materialsDifferent

    The Road shop sells the Raviente, Violent Raviente and Berserk Raviente GX decorations for 20,000, 30,000 and 40,000 RdP each with no limit, the G Raviente materials for 200 RdP (a GX armour piece needs about 150 to 175, so 30,000 to 35,000 RdP, about one siege's worth of party Road), the Raviente Ruby and Sapphire for 1,000 and Divine Power Gems, from GR 1, and the Extreme Raviente GX decorations for 60,000 RdP each from GR 900, so the weekly-limit tables above do not apply here (from our shop data; not tested in game). Its full stock is on Sources.
