Gear and skills

The fourteen weapons, sigils, armour, decorations, skills and Transcend.

Gear

Frontier's equipment is two progressions that meet at G Rank. Below it you craft from monster materials and push armour up with spheres; at G Rank weapons gain levels to 50 and swap decorations for sigils, while armour becomes a five-piece puzzle of points, slots and set bonuses. Endgame sets are built backwards from the skills you need.

The fourteen weapons

Each class applies a multiplier to true raw for the attack number on screen, which is why a Great Sword reads in the thousands and a Bow in the hundreds. Compare classes by true raw, never by displayed attack.

Switch Axe F unlocks at GR; Tonfa needs a chain of unlock quests first. Styles decide the rest: Heaven and Storm at HR5, Extreme from G Rank, and Extreme is the default for high-level play on every class.

Sword and Shield. Fastest class and best raw element/status applicator. Full item use with the weapon drawn, and low commitment keeps you on a hitzone.

Dual Swords. Demon Mode trades stamina for attack and speed, and the class is built around staying in it. Extremely high hit count, so element and status convert best.

Great Sword. Charge, release, sheathe. It lives on prediction rather than pressure, and its parry turns the matchup into a timing exercise.

Long Sword. Spirit gauge maintenance is the class: filling it upgrades attacks and funds mobility. Extreme style adds a teleport that makes repositioning nearly free.

Hammer. Head hunter. Charge attacks build KO, and a hammer not hitting the head is a worse Great Sword. Movement while charging is the real defensive tool.

Hunting Horn. A Hammer that buffs. Songs play through attacks, so uptime and party support are the same action; you hold Attack Up while contributing impact damage.

Lance. Guard, counter, repeat. Almost no mobility, the best block in the game, and a shield that can reflect damage. Positioning is decided before the monster moves.

Gunlance. Shelling ignores much of what makes other classes care about hitzones, and Wyvern Fire is a burst on cooldown. You manage shelling level; the rest is a slower Lance that explodes.

Tonfa. G Rank only and the most mobile blademaster. EX and combo gauges reward uninterrupted aggression, kicks give aerial options, and Ryuuki finishers change by body part.

Switch Axe F. G Rank only. Two modes, a phial meter refilled by reloading or guarding, and a burst loop: build, spend, reset. Stunning Blast sigils add KO.

Magnet Spike. Frontier's own class. Magnetism pulls you to a monster or it to you, giving gap-closing no other blademaster has, at a gunner's multiplier.

Heavy Bowgun. Damage class, paid for with immobility and recoil. Heat Cannon adds a beam to a deep shot roster, and critical distance is worth more here than anywhere.

Light Bowgun. Rapid Fire is the identity: one trigger pull, several bullets, paid for in recoil and reload. Ultra Rapid Fire dumps a whole stack in one volley.

Bow. Charge levels, shot types and coatings. The one class that wants Auto-Reload as a core skill, and it lives at critical distance — too close loses most of its damage.

Sigils

G Rank weapons use sigils instead of decorations. Slots are triangular: some replace decoration slots outright, others are hybrid slots taking either a decoration or a sigil, and a weapon holds up to three. They are crafted at the Cat Smith, the NPC who rolls random Gou weapons.

Variable-value sigils stack; ones that add or enhance an ability or motion have a fixed effect however many you socket. Weapon-specific sigils look good and mostly are not: they buff one move, and optimal play often skips it. The Dual Swords Rush Slash and Frontflip Slash sigils do nothing in Extreme Demon Mode, and the Great Sword Guard Slash sigil dies when Extreme style removes the move. Aim for Attack or Elemental plus one or two sigils for moves you actually spam.

Only the highest roll of a weapon's "Up" sigil applies: +14 and +12 are worth +14, not +26. A shelling Gunlance should chase Lv9 shelling first, since shelling level is a flat step rather than a percentage. Rarity Change lowers raw, element and status to set rarity — a tool for hardcore-quest limits. At the top end, Zenith sigils are timed buffs with duration and cooldown rolls, and area sigils drop a coloured dome granting attack, element, affinity, status, stun, healing or resistance.

Armour, rarity and the upgrade path

Armour is five pieces — head, chest, arms, waist, legs — each carrying skill points, slots and defence. Crafting happens at the Blacksmith; with a Partner, the first menu asks whether the gear is for you or them.

Below G Rank the path is sphere-based: levels 2 to 6 consume Armour Spheres, and Lv7 opens a second tier (F, FX, HS) that consumes items again. Sphere tiers follow rank, from plain and + at HR1–2 up through Advanced, Hard, Heavy, True and Strong, with Divine dropping only from high-tier rewards. G Rank armour drops spheres and upgrades on materials through G, GF and GX at seven levels per tier.

Rarity is a stat, not a colour. Hardcore quests pay bonus HRP, SRP, zeny and GCP only while your weapon sits inside the Book of Secrets rarity limit, which is why low-rarity builds and Rarity Change sigils exist. G Rank armour gives +30 true raw and raises the skill limit from 10 to 11 at three pieces and 12 at five. Gou armour instead gives Muscle Boost: one skill a full level while above a health threshold, 100% at one piece down to 70% at five.

The families chain: Gou at HR5, Heavenly Storm at SR100, Supremacy at SR300, then G Supremacy, Burst and Origin from GR1. Each step adds per-piece attack and attribute, cuts the hardcore rarity requirement by one per piece, and at two pieces (one for Origin) upgrades blademaster sharpness a colour or boosts gunner critical distance and Power Coatings. Origin is the one gunners never skip, because those gunner buffs apply to every G Rank weapon. Exotic armour carries a skill always on regardless of limit, counts as G Rank pieces once upgraded, and needs GR500 to pass G. Zenith armour runs Z, ZF, ZY and ZX at GR200, 400, 600 and 800, with premium pieces going to ZP. Tower (Tenrou) armour instead takes decorations imitating other families' bonuses.

On Hunsterverse: armour whose only source was a paid or premium channel is not obtainable here, since there is no cash shop and no paid service. Billing and lottery gear has to come from the server's own quest set instead.

Decorations

Decorations fill every decoration slot, at G Rank as much as below it. Standard ones are blacksmith crafts from ordinary materials worth about five points, often with a negative elsewhere — the classic Artisan jewel is +2 Artisan, +2 Auto-Reload, −1 Adrenaline. They stop being interesting past HR7.

G decorations are stronger versions, improved from standard jewels or built from dedicated materials. When one needs a specific jewel item, that item exists only to unlock the craft and drops from one dedicated quest. G Rank decorations are mostly direct crafts from a couple of low-percentage carves plus a GHC carve, and most are stocked in the Road, Festival or GCP stores.

The old route still exists: refining a Lv7 armour piece destroys it for one decoration. G pieces give G gems, GF pieces GF gems, GX pieces GX1 to GX5 by slot — head, torso, arms, waist, legs in that order. Gem skills mirror the piece destroyed, so a Silver Rathalos GX helmet becomes a GX1 gem carrying that helmet's skills. Gallery gems come from gallery layouts and quest rewards, not crafting.

Armour skills

Skills activate on points, up to 10 at once and 11 or 12 with G Rank armour. Every skill also has an invisible priority, and a set can silently lose one to something that outranks it — Quick Charge is the usual victim. Hybrid skills bundle several effects into one slot but still occupy it when greyed out.

Muscle Boost raises one active skill a full level while the armour's health and stamina condition holds — 10 to 30 points for free, and the reason Gou pieces stay relevant long after their raw stops impressing.

The multipliers define sets: Attack Up and Strong Attack for raw, Expert and Issen for affinity, Razor Sharp and Artisan to hold a colour, Exploit Weakness and Dissolver because hitzone values multiply everything else, and Focus or Charge Attack Up on anything with a charge. Encourage is the cheapest party buff, and Artillery is mandatory on a shelling Gunlance.

Transcend

Transcend unlocks at HR3 as a timed self-buff carried like a consumable. It starts nearly empty and charges as you fight; the icon fills from grey to a flashing hunter, and using it grants an aura, buffs and altered hit animations. Settings live under Status → Secret Ceremony Status.

The offensive track is Attribute Pulse, one tree per element plus raw, ten levels each. Melee elemental runs 110% at level 0 to 200% at level 10; raw runs 101% to 113%; ranged 101% to 113%. Costs climb from 1,500 points for the first level to 15,500 for the last.

The defensive track is broader. Enduring Cloak gives +50 defence and +10 resistance at level 0 up to +800 and +100. Concentration pre-charges the gauge from 0% to 25%. Courage extends the state from 60 to 120 seconds. Stronghold grants super armour, Strider movement speed, and Recovery heals nearby players and NPCs.

Points come from Ancient Hunting Books — 500 red, 1,000 blue, 3,000 dark, awarded at milestones in Conquests, the Tower, Festival and Caravan — or from taking a weapon of the matching element on quests, which feeds that tree directly.

After enough hits the icon becomes a carving knife and you can fire an Elemental Burst. Fire deals damage and staggers; water softens hitzones for a long time; ice freezes the monster but you cannot cut the tail while it holds; thunder paralyzes; dragon stuns and can KO; raw hits hard and adds a flash. Immunity forces the burst to raw, and hybrid elements pick by an internal hierarchy. Status only lands if the monster can be afflicted and is in a staggerable frame.

On Hunsterverse: the element-feeding path always works, since it only needs you to hunt with the right weapon. The book path depends on Conquests, Tower, Festival and Caravan being in the server's quest set; where those were scheduled live events, treat the books as unavailable rather than as a weekly chore.
