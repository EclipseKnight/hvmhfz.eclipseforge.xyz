    What is the Sky Corridor?

    The Sky Corridor, or the Tower, is a tower of floors to climb, with treasure chests, Tower Medals, the materials for Tower weapons and armour, and Duremudira fights. On Hunsterverse it is open since 3 November 2026.

    How to get in

    • Join a guild first. The game reports a cleared tower quest to the server only for guild members, so hunters without a guild are not counted here, in the town's progress or in the present box.
    • From about Hunter Rank 31, a town event introduces the Tower. Take a tower quest at the tower counter.
    • Each cleared tower quest counts as one tower clear in its district, plus the floors you climbed, whether you killed Duremudira or left by the broken wall (Return to Mezeporta). A party that kills Duremudira also adds one Duremudira kill to the district's goal; the board's Duremudira kills are your own.
