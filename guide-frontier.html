Frontier handbook

Everything Frontier Z, from a first hunt to Zenith, rewritten for this server.

What this is

A handbook for Monster Hunter Frontier Z, rewritten for this server. It is based on Fist's guide, a 34-page reference written for Capcom's live servers between 2007 and 2022, reorganised so you can look something up instead of scrolling a spreadsheet.

Everything here is accurate for Frontier Z as a game. Where our server does something differently, the section says so in an On Hunsterverse note.

Read it in four chapters

  • Getting started and progression: how a hunt works, Hunter Rank, Style Rank, G Rank, Zenith.
  • Gear and skills: the fourteen weapons, sigils, armour, decorations, skills and Transcend.
  • What there is to do: quests, festivals, Diva Defense, Hunter's Road, Caravan, Raviente.
  • Reference: house and partner, items and boxes, the monster list, gathering maps, tools.

What is different on Hunsterverse

Live serversHere
Company-run event calendarNo calendar: what exists is the server's own quest set, and events appear when that content does
Japanese quest text with a paid English optionEnglish titles, objectives and descriptions throughout
New Road rotation set by the company every two weeksRotations every two weeks, but a quest party picks its Road with !road, up to four hunters, and old Roads stay playable with their own boards
Content created only by CapcomPlayers submit quest files for admin approval; approved quests can be played, sandbox tests have every reward stripped
Character progress tracked only in the saveThe site tracks playtime, hunts, weapon use, quest outcomes and Road placings
Paid services and a cash shopNone

How to use it with the site

  • Hunters and leaderboards show what the server has recorded.
  • Quests is the full quest catalogue, with English text and tracked outcomes.
  • Roads guide covers the in-game Road commands and what the rest of the party sees.
  • The Discord /guide command posts the short version for new players.
