    Food and Bentos

    Every Hunter Bento the game can make. Pick two ingredients at the food shop and the whole party gets the bento's buff on the next quest you post. Here are all 990 pairs in each season, the 16 SR bentos, and where to buy every ingredient on this server.

    Bentos SR bentos Ingredients How it works

    The buff depends on the season of the quest you post: the same two ingredients can help in one season and hurt in another. The quest card shows the season on its fifth page.

    Recommended for the Warm Season

    All bentos

    The numbers: what the whole party gets when the quest starts. Health +50: your health bar is 50 longer; −40: 40 shorter, which some hunters want for Adrenaline skills, which work while health is low. Stamina +25: your stamina bar is 25 longer. Attack +5 and Defence +20: added to your attack and defence. A dash means no change. Best overall puts health first, then attack, stamina and defence (1.5 × health + stamina + 10 × attack + defence ÷ 2); with Health set to "Lowered" it counts a lower health as better.

    SR bentos are made only from the eight SR ingredients: one of the four on top with one of the four on the side. They are the same in every season and stronger than any normal bento. The ingredient on top gives the attack and defence, the one on the side the health and stamina.

    How to get SR ingredients here

    • Food shop, at GR 999: the food shop sells all eight to hunters at GR 999 (the game says so itself: "SR ingredients ... only for GR999 hunters").
    • Hunting Road shop: its Other tab has Millennium Rice, Silver Liver, Tuna Blowfish and Thin Wyvern Cut (one of each), its Special tab Tuna Blowfish, Rainbow Lettuce and Queen Parsley (from GR 1), paid in Road points.
    • Quests: the daily quest Hunt Support: Secret Ingredients rewards all eight; it is the only way to Bitter Cheese and Dancing Spice below GR 999.
    • Guild treasure hunts: Millennium Rice, Silver Liver, Tuna Blowfish and Thin Wyvern Cut (treasure hunt rewards).
    • The GCP shop and the Hunter Festival prizes sell no bento ingredient here. Each ingredient's own page lists every place to get it: see the Ingredients tab.

    The 53 ingredients, where to buy each here, and the shop's full price (the food shop sells for half on some days). "Bentos" shows every bento made with it. An ingredient no shop sells comes from quests, gathering, fishing or monsters: its page lists every place.

    Food shop
    The food NPC in town (and the one in the Caravan area): "Buy Ingredients" or "Pack Bento". Some ingredients are only there on its extra-stock days, and the SR ingredients only for GR 999 hunters.
    Guild store cat
    The store cat in your Guild Hall, under "Hunter Bento", once your guild is rank 4 or more.
    Course shop
    A shop list for hunters with a paid course. Every account here is said to have the Premium course; which shopkeeper sells it has not been checked here.

    Buying and using a bento

    1. Talk to the food NPC in town (or the store cat in your Guild Hall) and choose Pack Bento.
    2. Pick the first ingredient, then a different second one. The list shows the ingredients you carry and the ones the shop sells that day.
    3. Post a quest: the bento is set for it (a rice ball icon on the posting screen), and everyone in the party gets the buff when the quest starts.
    4. The game remembers your bento between quests. Each bento you make goes on a quick list: while you post a quest, X on a controller or Z on a keyboard packs one from it again. Change it when the season changes.

    More pictures in the Frontier guide: Bentos (party buffs).

    Good to know

    • The same two ingredients always make the same bento in a season, whichever you pick first.
    • The season matters. Each ingredient helps different things in the Warm, Cold and Breeding Seasons, so the best pair changes with the season of the quest.
    • The dish name says nothing about the buff. There are only 16 dish names, and the game gives one to every pair by a fixed rule, so two bentos with the same name can do very different things. Look at the numbers, not the name.
    • Some pairs lower everything. For about one pair in eight the game turns the buff around, so good ingredients can make a bad bento: Fist Rice with Gold Extract lowers health and attack. Check a pair here before you buy.
    • Health and stamina are the length of your bars when the quest starts. Attack and defence are added to your own. A bento gives at most +50 health, +50 stamina, +5 attack and +20 defence; SR bentos up to +15 attack and +80 defence.
    • Read from the game's own data, and checked against the Frontier guide's table and a Japanese fan site's lists: all match except one row of the old guide, which the game proves wrong (fixed in our guide).
