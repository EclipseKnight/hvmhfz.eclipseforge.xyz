    Quests, Food Buffs and Bentos

    How quests are posted and found, who hands them out, what each quest menu holds, how to read a quest card, and how to pack bentos (food buffs) before you go.

    Bentos (party buffs)

    The basics

    A lunchbox, or bento, is a meal that gives a buff to everyone in your party. You set it when you post a quest. There are many foods you can buy and many ways to combine them.

    You pack a bento at the food NPC: Mama Grocer, the food store in town (the food store in the Caravan area works the same), or the store cat in your Guild Hall (our client also calls it Gacha Felyne; its Hunter Bento menu needs guild rank 4 or more). Mama Grocer's menu in our client: Hunter Bento, Buy, Sell, Buy to Box and Bento Settings.

    Once you have made a meal, it is added to a quick list. While you post a quest, press X on a controller or Z on a keyboard to prepare any meal on that list at once.

    The game remembers your meal between quests. If you chose the right food the first time, you do not need to choose again until the season changes.

    Every pair of ingredients and its buff in each season, the SR bentos and where to buy each ingredient on our server are on the Food and Bentos page.

    Combinations

    The tables below list the basic combinations. Always post quests with buffs at least as good as these when you can. Each meal is two ingredients. Which meal is best depends on the season of the quest (warm, cold or breeding).

    Fist Rice + Round Egg+50 HP / +3 Attack / +20 Defense
    ColdFist Rice + Fatty Tomato+50 HP / +50 Stamina
    Ancient Beans + Gold Extract+50 HP / +25 Stamina / +5 Attack

    Adrenaline combinations lower your HP so that Adrenaline skills trigger straight away.

    SR food

    SR food gives far stronger effects than normal food. The Queen Parsley combinations give -60 Health and +50 Stamina, with Attack Up and Defense Up from the main ingredient. That puts your party straight into Adrenaline range for the hunt. Other combinations are made for normal, non-Adrenaline quests and give +50 Health and more. All 16 SR bentos are on Food and Bentos.

    Hunsterverse The food shop (Mama Grocer) sells the eight SR ingredients to GR 999 hunters. Below GR 999 they come from quests, the Hunting Road shop and guild treasure hunts (table below). Neither the GCP shop nor the Hunter Festival prizes sell any bento ingredient here, so the original guide's GCP and Festival Points advice does not apply.

    Server types

    The world you are on can change which quests you see. On the Capcom servers the world list had five tabs (HR1-4, HR5 and G Rank, Private Towns, Private Bars and Cheat Servers) and the guide sent players to the town entrance to change server. Our world list is different, so those tabs and that advice do not apply here.

    Different. Hunsterverse has its own worlds: Newbie, Normal (EN), Normal (ES), Cities, Tavern, MezFes and Test (the pre-release), not the five Capcom types. The Return World was removed on 4 Nov and MezFes is open every day. See Worlds. What the Newbie world offers beyond the normal worlds was not checked.

    Quest givers

    The names below are our client's own. The community guide called the guild receptionists by colour; those names are not in our client.

    Guild Master

    The Guild Master is a town NPC. Her menu in our client: Hiden Book Menu, Transcend Menu, Trial Menu, Goocoo Quests (the Gu-ku quests, by your garden's progress: see Getting your first Gu-ku), GSR Menu and Koryo Menu. As the guide says, she hands out Gu-ku quests and manages Style Rank. Her Koryo Menu gives a Hunting Medal (the menu calls it a Koryo Medal) each time you reach a monster's hunt count, and you trade the medals at the Combo Shop Master for amulets (charms).

    Series Quest Counter

    The Series Quest Counter gives the story quests, one series at a time (Beginners and Veteran tabs), and trades some items for others (its Item exchange). The very first tutorial quests are at the rookie area's counter, "Accepting the first quest": Starter Quests, then Hunter Quests for HR1-30, HR31-99 and HR100+. Trading with the Series Quest Counter is the only way to get some materials.

    General Quest Counter and G Rank Quest Counter

    The General Quest Counter gives all quests below G Rank once you are past the rookie quests: Hunter Quests, Event, Daily, Special and Clan Quests, and the Caravan Quests row. The G Rank Quest Counter gives the G Rank quests and needs G Rank. They stand in one row in Mezeporta (see Menus and Town NPCs).

    Daily quests are a row on both counters (Daily Quests, see below). During Diva Defense its own screen gives the Diva Defense quests (Int. Quests, Split Route Quests and G Rank Int. Quests, see Diva Defense).

    Different. The counters and their rows are client content and work the same, but the guide's colour nicknames for the guild girls are not our client's names. Which counter offers a quest here is on each quest's page under "Where to take it on Hunsterverse". The Guild Master's jobs and the arena quest counter were not checked.

    Quest menus

    Not every quest type is always available. You unlock some of them as you reach the rank needed to sign them, and a row with nothing in it is hidden. Only 1,952 of our 9,152 quests are offered: at a counter, or by the Guild Master for the Gu-ku quests. Each quest's page says where to take it, if it is offered.

    The General Quest Counter menu has these rows: G Experience Quests (from HR 100), Event Quests, Daily Quests, Hunter Quests, Other Quests, Special Quests, Clan Quests, Caravan Quests, VS. Quests, Hunting Competition, Winners Quests, My Missions Quests, Navi Recommended Quests, Favorites, Quest History and Quest Search. Under Hunter Quests there is a row for Urgent Quests, one for each of ★1 (HR1) to ★6 (HR6), and ※ Exotic Quests. The G Rank Quest Counter has its own menu (G Hunter Quests with ★1 to ★7 and more, G Urgent Quests, Zenith Quests, Conquest・Shiten Quests and so on).

    Urgent quests appear only when needed: the Urgent Quests row under Hunter Quests shows once you have done the band's key quests. There is no HR6 urgent. Five urgent quests are offered here, from HR1 to HR5 (for example [Urgent] Great Forest Lord Awakens at HR2). On the G Rank Quest Counter the G Urgent Quests row holds one offered quest, The Towers Black Fox！. The Shantien quest that takes you into G Rank is not an urgent here: it is the G Rank Promotion Exam, Legend Soaring the Skies, which the G Rank receptionist offers at HR 999.

    Other Quests

    The General Quest Counter's Other Quests row holds Gear Acquisition Quests and Hunting Skill Quests (it also has Returning Clan Quests and Special Clan Quests). The community guide described a longer list of tabs here. Most of them are not in our client's counters:

    • Gear Acquisition Quests (called Special Material Quests in the guide; the G Rank Quest Counter has its own row of the same name) hold the long-term quests and quests for weapon, armour and decoration materials. The guide's Special Material quests themselves are not offered here.
    • Hunting Skill Quests (15 offered) are the Hunt Technique quests. Party members compete for the most Contribution Points, scored from what each does in the hunt (fainting and some items take points off), and your final points decide your rank in that quest.
    • Notes Quests (Memo Quests) are not a tab here. The 12 Memo quests that give items such as Neodymium Note, used on things like Tower or FX armour pieces, are in Special Quests > Premium Quests, for example Blue Kut-Ku and Boron Memo.
    • G Deco Quests are not offered here: there is no "G Deco Quests" tab on any counter, and none of those quests can be taken.
    • Skill Cuff Quests (materials for Skill Cuffs, see House and AI Companions) are not offered here either.
    • Long Term Quests are not a tab here. The 39 Long-Term Event quests are under Other Quests > Gear Acquisition Quests and Special Quests > Paw Coins/Yearly Benefits.

    Scheduled Quests

    This section holds quests on a changing schedule, or otherwise notable enough to deserve their own section.

    • Event Quests (the guide's Active Events) are quests tied to events running on the server. There is one row on the General Quest Counter and one on the G Rank Quest Counter. Hardcore quests (the guide's Gou quests) are in Hunter Quests > ★5, for example Hardcore Gleam! Garuba Daora.
    • Exotic Quests are quests against mainline imports such as Zinogre or Deviljho, for example Peerless Hunter (Zinogre) and Ravenous Tyrant King (Deviljho). They are in General Quest Counter > Hunter Quests > ※ Exotic Quests, and G Rank ones in G Rank Quest Counter > G Hunter Quests > ※ G Exotic Quests.
    • Daily Quests are a row on both counters. Each one uses a Daily Quest Right, which you claim at the General Store (Daily Special). The guide says the claim also gives NP; we did not check that.

    The live event quest pool on our server is listed on Events, and every quest has an English entry in the Quest catalog.

    Special Quests

    General Quest Counter > Special Quests holds Promotional, HR Ranking Rewards, Paw Coins/Yearly Benefits, Weapon Souls & Hiden, Superior Quests, Premium Quests and Luxury Quests.

    • HR Ranking Rewards are three one-time quests for reaching HR5, HR6 and HR7: Beyond the Peak [HR5], Beyond the Peak [HR6] and Beyond the Peak [HR7]. Deliver a Map on your own. The rewards are Transcend Proofs and 30k Cheques (not the earring materials the guide describes).
    • Paw Coins/Yearly Benefits quests give materials for anything that needs PawCoin items. The guide links them to cashing in 48 weeks' worth of stamps: our server does trade 48 weekly HL stamps for one Yearly Sub Ticket (and 8 stamps for 5 HL Tkt or 5 EX Tkt).
    • Weapon Souls & Hiden has one Weapon Soul Quest for each weapon type, for example Weapon Soul Quest [Sword & Shield]. Each needs a Secret Soul Crystal to accept and gives Weapon Souls of that one weapon type for delivering a Map. Choose with care: pick the quest for the weapon you want.
    • Superior Quests need Superior Tkt (General Quest Counter > Special Quests, and G Rank Quest Counter > Special Quests). On the Capcom servers you got these from the real-money lottery. Here the Hunting Road shop sells them (Special tab) and the Series Quest Counter trades for them.
    • Premium Quests need a premium course and have higher than usual rewards. The guide's author rates them poorly and says premium should not be used just to reach them. Every account here has the Premium course (not tested in game), and our quest catalogue holds 58 Premium Quests, 44 of them offered (see Quests).
    • Gathering Quests are on the G Rank Quest Counter (G Hunter Quests > ※ Gathering Quests): 45 G★1 gathering quests on the field maps. The General Quest Counter has no Gathering Quests row.

    Different. The menus are client content and our rows are the ones above, in our client's words. Our server holds 9,152 quest files (see Quests), including Premium, Zenith, Supremacy, Hardcore, Musou and Exotic quests, but only 1,952 can be taken here (at a counter, or from the Guild Master for the Gu-ku quests). The G Deco Quests and Special Material tabs are not reachable here, and their quests are not offered. Each quest's page shows where to take it.

    Ranking points

    Finishing quests gives you several kinds of points, depending on which NPCs you bring on the quest.

    PointsHow you earn themWhat they do
    Partner Rank PointsBased on the quest's GRP or HRP valueRaise your partner's PR, up to PR999
    Partner Weapon Rank PointsBased on the quest's GRP or HRP valueRaise your partner's Weapon Rank, up to 999
    Partnya Rank PointsBased on the quest's GRP or HRP valueRaise your Partnya's Rank, up to 999
    Guild Contribution PointsClearing quests while you have a Rasta or Legendary Rasta contracted (the guide says 1 per minute of quest time)Spendable points
    G Rank PointsBased on the quest's GRP valueRaise your GR rating, up to GR999
    G Style Rank PointsBased on the quest's GRP valueRaise your GSR rating, up to GSR999 per weapon

    You can gain points faster by going on GHC hunts, using certain Sigil Skills, listening to the Diva Song, using the Log In Boost, or using Premium.

    Normal non-G HC quests with a Rare-12 weapon, once you have reached G Rank, should multiply all of your NPC ranking point gains.

    Works the same. Points work the same and pay at the normal rate, because every quest reward multiplier is 1.0 here. Guild Contribution Points buy Diva Defense items here: the GCP shop holds 135 of them, and the Diva shop sells potions and drugs for GCP (see Currencies).

    The quest card

    The quest card appears when you hover over a quest in the quest list. It has 8 pages and every page has useful information. The ones you will use most are page 1 (core details), page 2 (money rewards), page 3 (material rewards), page 5 (season and time) and page 8 (location).

    The other pages show smaller details:

    • Page 4 shows the preset armour for quests that have one (head, chest, arms, waist). Armour and weapon are only preset on some event quests.
    • Page 6 lists which weapon types are allowed. Faded weapon types are banned. Weapon-restricted quests block NPCs other than Legendaries.
    • Page 7 lists banned weapon types. For example, in recent hunting contests you can only use Origin weapons, so every other weapon type is listed here.
    • Page 8 also shows the standard quest flavour text, such as the contractor and background info.

    Works the same. The quest card is client content and works the same. Names on it are in English here.

    Objectives

    Quest goals are made from these parts:

    • Deliver, Hunt, Slay, Capture, Repel and Break Part (the quest text says "Break" or "Sever" the part).
    • Break Part objectives can name many parts. The guide lists: Wing, Head (including crest), Tail, Beak, Horn, Shell, Chest, Pincers, Tentacles, Ears, Claws, Fin, Head and Body, and Back.

    Sub-objectives

    Like some mainline games, Frontier often has sub-objectives. Each has its own reward pool of up to 4 slots, in blue or green slots on the first page of rewards. Completing one plays a jingle and shows it as completed on your quest status page.

    Finishing a sub-objective lets you end the quest at once. Sleep in the bed at the Basecamp and choose the second option. This matters at G Rank, where not every map has a gathering quest but you may still need to gather. Killing a handful of minions is much better than fighting a large monster or delivering eggs.

    Works the same. Areas, objectives and sub-objectives are client content and work the same.

    Searching for quests

    Choose Quest Search in a quest counter's menu (the last rows are Favorites, Quest History and Quest Search) to open the search. You can search by typing in the main text search or the reward string search, or just use the preset options. You can filter by monster, location, monster type, HR limit, flags, HRP reward and zeny reward.

    For English quest names and objectives on our server, use the Quest catalog; its monster list is on Monsters.

    Different. Quest search is a client menu and works the same. Quest and monster names are English here, so the original guide's advice about pasting Chinese text and its Google Document of search terms were removed. Use our Quests and Monsters pages to find names.

    Advertising quests

    The usual format for a quest advert is: @here / Quest Type / Monster Name / other info / @re. If you can paste into the game, you only need to paste the right text into the chat field. The original guide gave the Chinese shorthand of the Taiwanese players; here it is in English.

    General terms

    TermMeaning
    @hereMakes a link to where you are on the game servers
    @reAdds the total number of slots left for the quest
    FoodHave a food buff. Useful with numbers for Adrenaline, such as -50 HP or -60 HP
    Break HeadBreak the monster's head
    Cut TailCut the monster's tail
    Help wantedSeeking help
    Bring Para TonfaBring Paralysis Tonfas. Other statuses and weapons use the same format, for example Bring Poison Duals
    SR Adrenaline foodAn SR bento with an Adrenaline buff
    Hardcore or HCA Hardcore quest (the guide's Gou)
    ExoticA quest from the Exotic Quests list

    You can also just write HR with a number after it to advertise the rank.

    Conquest terms

    Conquest quests are on the G Rank Quest Counter, under Conquest・Shiten Quests (Lv1, Lv200, Lv1000, Lv9999, Shiten Quests, Adv Shiten Quests and About Shiten Quests).

    • Lv200 and similar: the level of the monster. This is needed when advertising a Conquest, because you have to be at or below the host's level to advance your own levels.
    • +10 / +15: how many levels the host gains if they do not cart.

    Examples

    • @here Hardcore Garuba Daora, Cut Tail, food @re! means: Hardcore Garuba Daora (Hardcore Gleam! Garuba Daora), requesting Tail Cut, have food buff.
    • @here G Exotic Zinogre, Break Head, Cut Tail, food, help wanted @re! means: G Rank Zinogre, Head Break and Tail Cut, have food buff, requesting help. (No offered G Rank Zinogre quest is on our counters today; the offered Zinogre quest is the HR5 Peerless Hunter.)

    Because there are no bans for English players, you can advertise with the monster's English name or a rough description, for example @here HR5 Zinogre! @re!.

    Unknown, not checked. Adverts are plain chat, so the format should work here. The shorthand in the table comes from the Taiwanese servers, and we have not checked whether players on our worlds use it or what @here and @re do on our server.

    Quest rewards

    After any quest you receive rewards based on the quest. They are always shown in the same format: a prompt to send items to the item pouch, send them to the item box, send everything to the item box, or sell the remainder, with a timer, and then a reward grid.

    Works the same. The reward screen works the same, and rewards pay at the normal rate: every quest reward multiplier is 1.0, with no extra carves.

    Caravan, Guild and Diva quests

    All information on Caravan is in Caravan.

    Guild Quests

    Guild Quests are a row of the quest counter, also reachable at a guild hall counter: General Quest Counter > Clan Quests > Quest Orders (75 offered) and Training Support Quests (10 offered, for example Guild Bond [Crystal Dragon]). Both rows need a Guild Ticket. Guild Quests give Guild Tickets (42 of our 85 Guild Quest files reward them), which you can spend on various pieces of equipment exclusive to them. As well as standard Guild Quests, you can add sub-quests with the Add Request row of Clan Quests. The guide says a second, smaller quest icon above your character's normal quest icon shows that a sub-quest is selected; we did not check that.

    Diva Quests

    Our client puts the Diva at the Prayer Fountain in Mezeporta Square (the gate to her area is on the steps leading to the square, as the picture shows); her story starts with the Attending Cat there. Her questline gives materials for Diva-exclusive equipment, which is extremely good. The first chapter is for Hunter Rank before Style Rank, the second needs Style Rank, and every chapter after that needs G Rank (our client's Diva Story tips say the same).

    You may have to talk to more people than the questline shows. See G Rank for the Diva Buffs, which you can only use after you reach G Rank.

    Different. Caravan scores post at quest end here, and since 4 Nov the Caravan rankings show real scores. The Diva questline is in English, and Diva Defense has run here since early November (new, not much played yet; its dates are on Events): Diva quests give extra points, and its rewards come in the later weeks (see Events). Guild Quest rows and Guild Tickets are checked above against our quest data; the sub-quest icon and the Diva chapters were not tested in game.
