    Items and Materials

    How to find where a material comes from, a quick list of the basic items, armour spheres, every combination recipe, and the special Frontier-only items, tickets and charms.

    Finding where a material comes from

    While you are crafting something and can see the list of required materials, press in the Left Stick or press 1 on the keyboard. The game highlights a material and shows where it comes from.

    The message always has the same shape: a rank tag, then where. In our client it reads, for example, "Found in [HR 1-2]" followed by an area, "Gathering" or "Mining", or "Obtain from" and a monster; shop items say "Trading: Npoints" or "Trading: GCP Exchange". Any carve marked HC needs Hardcore turned on (see Hardcore and Starting G Rank).

    The rank tags count Hunter Rank bands (HR 1 to HR 6), not your exact Hunter Rank. Each band covers:

    Band in the tagHunter Rank
    HR 1HR 1
    HR 2HR 2-30
    HR 3HR 31-50
    HR 4HR 51-99
    HR 5HR 100-299
    HR 6HR 300-998

    Area names used in these messages: Jungle, Desert, Swamp, Snowy Mountains, Volcano, Tower (the Ancient Tower), Forest and Hills, Great Forest, Gorge, Highlands, Tidal Island, Flower Field, Polar Sea, White Lake and Painted Falls. If the message does not help, look the item up on our Items page.

    Different. The hint works the same, with English names (they may differ slightly from the guide's); see the Items page and the monster field guide. Items marked "Trading: Npoints" cannot be bought here: the N Point shop has no items on our server. "Trading: GCP" items are not in our GCP shop either, which holds only Diva Defense rewards. The guide's pointer to the official site's event news does not apply.

    Ferias

    Ferias is a community database site that the original guide used. We do not link the site or the translation script it relied on.

    The database lets you type an item name and gives links, where available, to the relevant sites. If you read a name from a screenshot with OCR, the database usually still finds it even when some characters are wrong, as long as most are right. If there are clear mistakes, cut the text down to the characters you believe are correct. Partial matches are fine.

    You can also type a monster name you recognise. It lists the materials with that monster's name in them. From there you can click through to Ferias for rank requirements, drop percentages and more.

    Different. Ferias is an outside database that we do not link. For items, equipment and monsters, use our Items page and monster field guide; item names are in English in our client.

    Different. Armour spheres matter more here. Since client patch v1.19 (9 Nov) each upgrade level of Low and High Rank armour costs one Armor Sphere of the right tier instead of monster parts (HC and some sets also need a Secret Proof at a few levels), and making the next piece needs about a third fewer materials; G Rank, GS/GP, Zenith, FZ and SP armour are unchanged. The Hunting Road shop's Gatherables tab sells every tier, from Armor Sphere to Absolute Armor Sphere (10 to 90 RdP, see Sources). The guide's GCP, N Point and Festival Point prices are left out: those shops do not sell spheres here.     Combinations

    Frontier has two combination modes: 2 item and 3 item combinations. For example, you can make an Ancient Potion by combining either Immunizer + Kelbi Horn or Catalyst + Dragon Toadstool + Kelbi Horn. Items are not limited to two recipes, so one result can have several.

    Each line below is one recipe, with its percentage (the chance the combination works). Each recipe makes 1 item unless the line says otherwise. Some recipes carry an [Alchemy] or [Mission] mark in the guide, which the guide does not explain. Numbers are the guide's own recipe numbers.

    The guide's recipe table was flattened when it was saved, and the recipes below are rebuilt from it, then checked against our combining data (the client's own table, Sources). Item names are the game's own English ones, which differ from the names the guide used (for example Dash Juice for the guide's Power Juice, Crystalized Scale for its Dragon Herb, Dragonfell Berry for its Dragon Seed, Jumbo Bone for its Giant Bone, Stinkhopper for its Bughopper, Lg Bomb Arowana and Lg Burst Arowana for its large arrowana and large rumblefish). Two-ingredient recipes were matched to the client table one by one. The three-ingredient recipes cannot be read from it, so they are as the guide gave them (not checked in game). The percentages are the guide's too: our data does not hold them. Two-ingredient recipes the client table does not have were dropped (Raw Meat + Red Oil, Energy Drink + Ripe Mushroom, Piscine Liver + Ripe Mushroom, and five Mocha Pot rows). The BBQ recipes (110 to 117) are not in the client's combining table either. Recipes 136 to 138 (secret-area and book items with no English names) are left out.

    Special items

    These items either change the rewards of a quest when you take them along, or exist only in Frontier and not in the main games.

    Halk Potion

    The Halk Potion massively reduces the damage you take in quests. You can use a maximum of 5 each day, and you get one a day from the Halk area. See Halks. Hunsterverse The event quests Mosswine at the Edge and Miyashita P's Answers 1 (General Quest Counter > Event Quests) also reward Halk Potions and Mega Guts Tkts.

    Consumables and tools

    ItemWhat it does
    Sword Crystals (in game: items such as Power S.Crystal and Poison S.Crystal)Ammo for Blademaster weapons. They need their own skills, with three levels in each. Having the skills at +3 guarantees maximum damage or status output from the crystals. See Sword Crystals.
    ToxinA consumable that takes away 10 health when you use it. It is very useful for triggering Adrenaline, because that skill goes by your HP total and not your health percentage. So you can eat any meal that gives -50 health and be in Adrenaline range straight away. It does not reduce your maximum HP, so Vampirism and armour with HC regen will stop you keeping Adrenaline health levels. Hunsterverse Our item data has no item named Toxin; not checked in game.
    Starving Wolf Pot.It instantly sets your maximum Stamina to 25, which can trigger Starving Wolf. Hunsterverse The Hunting Road shop's Special tab sells the Starving Wolf Pot. for 20 RdP (GR 1).
    Adrenaline PotionIt instantly sets your health to 40, which can trigger Adrenaline. It does not reduce your maximum HP, so Vampirism will stop you keeping Adrenaline health levels. Hunsterverse No shop sells it here, and the item data shows no source we could count.
    Spirit DrinkIt instantly fills the Long Sword Spirit Meter. It is fairly niche, since the meter fills easily, and not common enough for regular use. Hunsterverse No shop sells it here, and the item data shows no source we could count.

    Mysterious Stones

    Mysterious Stones (the guide's name; we could not match it to an item in our data) are rare materials, fairly commonly used in older G Rank equipment. You can get them in two ways: feed Pariapuria and gather his vomit, or use Gu-ku Cooking. The guide strongly recommends Gu-ku Cooking over trying to make Pariapuria vomit. The steps are in Gu-ku Cooking.

    Medal charms

    You get these charms in two steps. Kill certain numbers of monsters and the Guild Master gives you Hunting Medals (the guide's Hunt Medals, with an option to claim and confirm rewards). Then trade the medals at a combination store for the charms below (the guide's Exchange Medals for Charms, with a Refund Charms option).

    The guide's author is not sure of the exact requirement for claiming these medals. It is likely to be having killed at least 10 of a single monster type, and the author would like confirmation.

    These charms always affect the quest you take them on, and they are not consumed. Be very careful not to discard or replace them while on a quest. The charms that cut the waiting time only work when you play solo, so they suit you if you are spamming quests and do not mind possibly losing carves.

    How the gathering charms work

    Every gathering spot has a fixed minimum and maximum number of gathers, and the gathering charms change these values. For example, the guide gives the insect spot in White Lake Area 1 a range of 4 to 8 gathers. With Gather.C. (Min+3) this becomes 7 to 8. With Gather.C. (Max+2) it becomes 4 to 10. So Min+3 is best for steady results and Max+2 is best for the highest possible return.

    Unknown, not checked. The charms work as the guide says, but we could not find how a player gets them here. Our data lists a Hunting Medal and the Haste C. items, yet no shop, quest reward or exchange gives them, and the guide's Guild Master and combination store menus are not in our client name list. Medal costs, gathering ranges and the "at least 10 of a monster" rule are the guide's and were not tested in game.
