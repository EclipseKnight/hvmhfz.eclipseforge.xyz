    Items and Materials

    How to find where a material comes from, a quick list of the basic items, armour spheres, every combination recipe, and the special Frontier-only items, tickets and charms.

    Finding where a material comes from

    While you are crafting something and can see the list of required materials, press in the Left Stick or press 1 on the keyboard. The game highlights a material and shows where it comes from. On the Taiwanese client the monster and place names were in Chinese, so players looked them up elsewhere.

    The message always has the same shape. For example, "Obtainable from [HR1~99] [Kelbi]" means the material comes from Kelbi at that rank range. Any carve marked HC needs Hardcore turned on (see Hardcore and Starting G Rank).

    The HR and SR ranges in these messages are still the ones from G9. The modern ranges are:

    Rank shownCurrent range
    HR1SR or HR1
    HR2SR or HR2-31
    HR3SR or HR32-50
    HR4SR or HR51-99
    HR5SR or HR100
    HR6SR300

    Words to look for

    SourceMeaning
    Gathering, Mining, Bug CatchingGathering spots, mining spots and insect spots
    Gook FarmComes from your Gook Farm
    N Point StoreBought with N points
    Conquest RewardA reward from a Conquest quest
    Time-limited or event itemsThe message points you to the official site's news and event pages to check

    Area names used in these messages: Jungle, Desert, Swamp, Snowy Mountains, Volcano, Tower, Forest and Hills, Great Forest, Gorge, Highlands, Tidal Island, Flower Field, Polar Sea, White Lake and Painted Falls. If the message does not help, use the other lists named in the guide.

    Different. The hint works the same, with English names (they may differ slightly from the guide's); see the item and equipment catalogue and the monster field guide. Materials marked as bought with N points come from a store with no entries on our server, and the pointer to the official site's news does not apply.

    Ferias

    Ferias is a community database site. There are two versions: a Japanese one, which is the original, and a less updated Taiwanese clone. The original guide used a translation script to read it in English; we do not link the site or the script.

    The database lets you type any English, Japanese or Chinese name. It then gives links, where available, to the relevant sites and shows all three versions of the name. If you read a name from a screenshot with OCR, the database usually still finds it even when some characters are wrong, as long as most are right. If there are clear mistakes, cut the text down to the characters you believe are correct. Partial matches are fine.

    You can also type a monster name you recognise. It lists the materials with that monster's name in them. From there you can click through to Ferias for rank requirements, drop percentages and more.

    Different. Ferias is an outside database that we do not link. For items, equipment and monsters, use our item and equipment catalogue and monster field guide; item names are in English in our client.

    Combinations

    Frontier has two combination modes: 2 item and 3 item combinations. For example, you can make an Ancient Potion by combining either Immunizer + Kelbi Horn or Catalyst + Dragon Toadstool + Kelbi Horn. Items are not limited to two recipes, so one result can have several.

    Special items

    These items either change the rewards of a quest when you take them along, or exist only in Frontier and not in the main games.

    Halk Potion

    The Halk Potion massively reduces the damage you take in quests. You can use a maximum of 5 each day, and you get one a day from the Halk area. See Halks.

    Special tickets

    TicketWhat it does
    Legendary Rasta TicketLets you take a Legendary Rasta on a quest without needing a Premium subscription. Given for completing Hunter Guide tasks and during events.
    Guts Ticket and Great Guts TicketStops you dying at 0 HP once. A Great Guts Ticket instantly refills your health to maximum. A Guts Ticket only stops you from dying. They work in situations where the Guts skill would not, including when you are on low HP.
    Poison, Paralysis and Sleep TicketsStop you from entering that status. They only cover the normal versions of the statuses, not the Zenith versions of Poison, Paralysis and Sleep.
    4,000 GRP TicketWhen taken on a quest, it gives you 4,000 GRP and GSRP after completion (gathering and simple quests are excluded), on top of the normal rewards. You can assign these points directly to your AI companions by talking to them, but the guide calls that fairly wasteful. Given as daily bonus rewards, for completing Hunter Guide tasks and during events. It does not stack with itself or other GRP tickets.
    20,000 GRP TicketThe same, but 20,000 GRP and GSRP. Given as daily bonus rewards, for completing Hunter Guide tasks and during events. It does not stack with itself or other GRP tickets.
    4,000 PRP TicketWhen taken on a quest, it gives your partner 4,000 PRP after completion (gathering and simple quests are excluded), on top of the normal rewards. You can assign these points directly to your AI companions by talking to them. The stamps that fill up after a quest grant these tickets, 1 or 3 at a time, when a card is completed. Claim them at the Blacksmith from the guild girl in glasses who wears brown and yellow, using her last option. It does not stack with other PRP tickets or with itself.
    20,000 PRP TicketThe same, but 20,000 PRP. Given as rewards for Hunter Navigation tasks, during events and as Caravan Festival rewards. It does not stack with other PRP tickets.

    The guide groups these as PRP, SRP, GRP and GSRP tickets.

    Consumables and tools

    Mysterious Stones

    Mysterious Stones are rare materials, fairly commonly used in older G Rank equipment. You can get them in two ways: feed Pariapuria and gather his vomit, or use Gook Cooking. The guide strongly recommends Gook Cooking over trying to make Pariapuria vomit. The steps are in Gook Cooking.

    Different. The items work the same, but course bonus items are not mailed out automatically: every account has the NetCafe courses, yet nothing delivers the drugs (the Guild Cat's daily gift was not checked). Our daily cafe bonus rewards exist, but we did not check what they hold.

    Medal charms

    You get these charms in two steps. Kill certain numbers of monsters and the Guild Master gives you medals (Hunt Medals, with an option to claim and confirm rewards). Then trade the medals at the Combination Store for the charms below (Exchange Medals for Charms, with a Refund Charms option).

    The guide's author is not sure of the exact requirement for claiming these medals. It is likely to be having killed at least 10 of a single monster type, and the author would like confirmation.

    These charms always affect the quest you take them on, and they are not consumed. Be very careful not to discard or replace them while on a quest. The charms that cut the waiting time only work when you play solo, so they suit you if you are spamming quests and do not mind possibly losing carves.

    How the gathering charms work

    Every gathering spot has a fixed minimum and maximum number of gathers, and the gathering charms change these values. For example, the Insect Spot in White Lake Area 1 has a range of 4 to 8 gathers. With a yellow minimum charm this becomes 7 to 8. With the black maximum charm it becomes 4 to 10. So yellow is best for steady results and black is best for the highest possible return.
