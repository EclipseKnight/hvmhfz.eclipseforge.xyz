Transcend

Transcend is a timed buff you charge up during quests and switch on like an item. This page covers its settings, every buff and level, how to earn upgrade points, and the Elemental Burst finisher.

What Transcend is

Transcend is a customisable, time-based buff that strengthens your hunter. It unlocks at HR3. Like the Wystone in Monster Hunter 4U, it appears as an item among your consumables. Unlike the Wystone, the Transcend state starts with little charge, and it has to power up over time during a quest.

You open the Transcend settings from the normal start menu. Choose Status, then Secret Ceremony Status. Menu names may differ slightly in our client. The settings window controls everything about the Transcend state: you can switch the mode on and off, and upgrade the buffs it gives.

Works the same. Transcend is game content and works the same. Menu names can differ slightly in our client.

Offensive buffs

There are six offensive buffs, called Attribute Pulse, one for each of Fire, Water, Ice, Thunder, Dragon and Raw. Each has 10 levels. An Attribute Pulse raises the damage of its element (or raw damage) by a set percentage.

Upgrading

You earn points to upgrade the buffs in two ways:

  • Use Ancient Hunting Books.
  • Take weapons of the matching element onto quests: Fire, Water, Ice, Thunder, Dragon, Raw and hybrid elements.

You get Ancient Hunting Books just by taking part in the cycling events (Conquests, Tower, Festival and Caravan). Different events give different books, and each book gives a different number of points. The books are given for reaching milestones in those events. For example, you might get 3 books for clearing 20 floors in the Tower. For these events, see Regular Events, including the Hunter Festival and the Caravan.

Using Transcend in a quest

When you enter a quest with Transcend on, you get a new item icon. It shows a hunter, and starts out light grey. As time passes, the grey area drains downwards. When it is ready, the icon is replaced by a yellow hunter on a flashing white background. That flashing always shows behind all your items, even if you have something other than the Transcend option selected.

Using the "item" puts you in the Transcend state. A special animation plays and your character gets an aura. While the aura lasts, you get the effects of every ability you have turned on, and your weapon attacks use new hit animations depending on the element you use.

After you land enough hits, a sound plays and the icon changes to a carving knife. This means you can use an Elemental Burst. Your character rushes forward with the knife, and when it hits the enemy, it sets off the burst that fits the element of your weapon and the monster you hit. A weapon with a combination element picks an effect based on which element hurt the monster most, or picks one at random. If the monster is immune to the element you dealt, the burst becomes Raw.

Works the same. Using Transcend works the same.

Elemental Bursts

You can use an Elemental Burst on a monster after it has taken enough hits during Transcend. The item icon becomes a knife, and the burst depends on the element of the weapon you are holding.

ElementElemental Burst effect
Attribute Pulse (Fire)Deals fire damage. Flame bursts can make most monsters stagger.
Attribute Pulse (Water)Deals low water damage and softens monster hitzones. It lasts a very long time.
Attribute Pulse (Ice)Freezes a monster solid, so it cannot move for a while. Tails cannot be cut during this state, and some monsters may resist freezing even when they take ice damage.
Attribute Pulse (Thunder)Deals thunder damage and paralyses monsters. It does not work on monsters that cannot be paralysed.
Attribute Pulse (Dragon)Deals dragon and stun damage, and can KO monsters.
Attribute Pulse (Raw)Deals large amounts of raw damage and causes a Flash Bomb effect.

The burst is forced to Raw if the monster takes no damage from the element you used. Hybrid elements such as Light have an order of preference for the element chosen. For example, Shantien weapons deal 30% Fire, 100% Water and 70% Thunder, but always pick Fire as the burst type if fire damage has been dealt.

Status effects only happen if the monster can normally be afflicted by them, and if you use the burst during frames in which the monster can be staggered. For example, an Ice burst during an uninterruptible attack animation will not freeze the monster. A Thunder burst on a monster that cannot be paralysed will not paralyse it.

Works the same. The bursts work the same.
