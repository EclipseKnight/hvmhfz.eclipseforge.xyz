        Event calendar

          One main and one side event at a time. Main event: Hunter Festival or Diva Defense, runs start on a Monday 03:00 UTC. Side event: Speedrun Cup or VS Bracket, runs start on a Thursday 15:00 UTC. A lighter bar is a run's tail (festival rewards, Diva week 3), which may run beside the next event of its lane. The game server reads the calendar every minute and writes each run's status and phases back; until it has, a run says "waiting for the game server". Times in texts are shown in your time zone (hover for UTC); the calendar strip and the time boxes are UTC.

          a gap: no main event, or no side event

          Point at the calendar to see what runs at that hour.

          A planned run can move: drag its bar (on a phone, hold it for half a second first), or drag an edge to change its length; it snaps to the hour, and to its lane's boundary (Monday 03:00, or Thursday 15:00 for the side event) and other runs' edges nearby (Shift: whole days at the lane's hour; Alt: hours only). With the keyboard: focus the bar, Left and Right move it by an hour (Shift: a day), Up and Down change its end (festival rewards by an hour, Cup and VS by a cycle), Escape cancels. Nothing is saved before you check the change below and press Save.

                          Notices

                            In game, a notice is one chat line to every player on every channel: at most 240 bytes, no emoji or accented letters (the game can't show them). On Discord it is a post by the bot in #mhfrontier, with mentions off. Every send is in the Audit tab. Admins only. Times are shown in your time zone (hover for UTC); the send-at box is UTC.

                                    Custom quests

                                    A submitted quest is listed for its author straight away (others can join when the author posts it). Publishing makes it public; rejecting removes it. A quest in testing expires after 14 days without activity (its author can resubmit it at any time); a published quest stays until you retire it. Custom quests pay their reward money; in testing they pay no item rewards and list none, and publishing makes them pay the author's rewards, so check those too. No leaderboard contribution.

                                    Manage (or a click on the row) opens one account: tick its roles and press Save, link or unlink its Discord account, set the chat flags and the patch channel, see all its characters and recent logins.                                     Balance

                                    The balance tables are not there yet: the API's db/balance.sql must run first.

                                    The weapon names are missing (data/weapons/ is not on this site yet), so moves and weapons are typed by number: weapon type, style, entry and record id.

                                    Every value the game uses for a move, a melee weapon or a ranged weapon can be changed here. Rows are the draft: nothing reaches players until it is published. Rows apply in order, wide rows (any) first. Publishing sends the table to every player online at once and to each player at login; a player in a quest gets it at the next town visit. It needs the client patch with server balance and the game server's balance support, neither live yet. Who may change it is set in the Permissions tab (by default only admins).

                                    The values

                                    Each cell shows the game's value; a changed one shows the new value with the game's struck through. Tap a cell to change it: Enter saves and goes down, Tab saves and goes right, Esc cancels. A whole number sets the value, x1.05 multiplies it; nothing, the game's value or the ↺ button puts it back. Every change goes to the draft at once; players get nothing until it is published.

                                    Each change needs a reason (the #weapon-balancing thread link). It is given to and every new change after it, until you change it above.

                                    Write the reason first.
