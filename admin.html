        Event calendar

          One main and one side event at a time. Main event: Hunter Festival or Diva Defense, runs start on a Monday 03:00 UTC. Side event: Speedrun Cup or VS Bracket, runs start on a Thursday 15:00 UTC. A lighter bar is a run's tail (festival rewards, Diva week 3), which may run beside the next event of its lane. The game server reads the calendar every minute and writes each run's status and phases back; until it has, a run says "waiting for the game server". All times on this tab are UTC.

          a gap: no main event, or no side event

          Point at the calendar to see what runs at that hour.

          A planned run can move: drag its bar (on a phone, hold it for half a second first), or drag an edge to change its length; it snaps to the hour, and to its lane's boundary (Monday 03:00, or Thursday 15:00 for the side event) and other runs' edges nearby (Shift: whole days at the lane's hour; Alt: hours only). With the keyboard: focus the bar, Left and Right move it by an hour (Shift: a day), Up and Down change its end (festival rewards by an hour, Cup and VS by a cycle), Escape cancels. Nothing is saved before you check the change below and press Save.

                          Notices

                            In game, a notice is one chat line to every player on every channel: at most 240 bytes, no emoji or accented letters (the game can't show them). On Discord it is a post by the bot in #mhfrontier, with mentions off. Every send is in the Audit tab. Admins only. All times on this tab are UTC.

                                The API sends each notice within 30 seconds of its time, once. If the API was down and a notice is more than 15 minutes late, it is marked missed instead of sent late.

                                Login notices

                                The pages players see at sign-in, in MHFML: <BODY> starts a line, <BR> ends it, <CENTER>, <SIZE_3>, colours <C_4> (highlight), <C_5> (warning), <C_7> (normal). The preview's colours are close, not exact. The same setting as Settings → Login notices.

                                  Event content

                                  The rows the game server reads for each event: festival trials and prizes, Diva rewards and coin shop, Cup and VS quests and prizes, campaigns, Sky Corridor rewards, event quests, gachas and shops. The API checks every change (limits, item ids against the site's item list) and refuses with reasons; a table is locked while players' claims or a running week point at its rows. Every change is in the Audit tab. Times are UTC.

                                    Custom quests

                                    A submitted quest is listed for its author straight away (others can join when the author posts it). Publishing makes it public; rejecting removes it. A quest in testing expires after 14 days without activity (its author can resubmit it at any time); a published quest stays until you retire it. Custom quests pay their reward money; in testing they pay no item rewards and list none, and publishing makes them pay the author's rewards, so check those too. No leaderboard contribution.

                                    Roles apply at the account's next request. Nobody can remove their own admin role, and the last admin cannot be removed. Search is case-insensitive and finds an account by its name or by any of its characters (matching characters are highlighted); account names themselves are case-sensitive. Each character links to its hunter page; deleted and never-played slots are listed but not linked. Link sets an account's Discord user id by hand, the same link /link makes (the Hunter role follows within 30 minutes); Unlink takes two clicks. Chat toggles are the in-game op (chat commands) and timer (quest timer) flags. Patch channel says which client patch the Mezeporta launcher gives the account: stable or pre: <slot> (only playtester, tester, moderator and admin accounts can have a pre-release; players join the playtest on their account page, and the Playtester button here gives or takes the role by hand); pick Stable or a slot and press Set, and the player can switch it too on the account page. A slot that is not out gives stable.
