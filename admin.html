      Event calendar

        One main and one side event at a time. Main event: Hunter Festival or Diva Defense, runs start on a Monday 03:00 UTC. Side event: Speedrun Cup or VS Bracket, runs start on a Thursday 15:00 UTC. A lighter bar is a run's tail (festival rewards, Diva week 3), which may run beside the next event of its lane. The game server reads the calendar every minute and writes each run's status and phases back; until it has, a run says "waiting for the game server". All times on this tab are UTC.

        a gap: no main event, or no side event

        Point at the calendar to see what runs at that hour.

        A planned run can move: drag its bar (on a phone, hold it for half a second first), or drag an edge to change its length; it snaps to the hour, and to its lane's boundary (Monday 03:00, or Thursday 15:00 for the side event) and other runs' edges nearby (Shift: whole days at the lane's hour; Alt: hours only). With the keyboard: focus the bar, Left and Right move it by an hour (Shift: a day), Up and Down change its end (festival rewards by an hour, Cup and VS by a cycle), Escape cancels. Nothing is saved before you check the change below and press Save.

                        Notices

                          In game, a notice is one chat line to every player on every channel: at most 240 bytes, no emoji or accented letters (the game can't show them). On Discord it is a post by the bot in #mhfrontier, with mentions off. Every send is in the Audit tab. Admins only. All times on this tab are UTC.

                                  Custom quests

                                  A submitted quest is testable by its author straight away, and only by them. Publishing makes it public; rejecting removes it. A quest in testing expires after 14 days without activity (its author can resubmit it for 30 days); a published quest stays until you retire it. Custom quests are sandbox content: no rewards, no zenny and no leaderboard contribution.

                                  Roles apply at the account's next request. Nobody can remove their own admin role, and the last admin cannot be removed. Search is case-insensitive and finds an account by its name or by any of its characters (matching characters are highlighted); account names themselves are case-sensitive. Each character links to its hunter page; deleted and never-played slots are listed but not linked. Link sets an account's Discord user id by hand, the same link /link makes (the Hunter role follows within 30 minutes); Unlink takes two clicks. Chat toggles are the in-game op (chat commands) and timer (quest timer) flags.

                                  Servers

                                  Status, the last restarts counter and how many players the game server reports. Logs hide secrets and mask player IPs. A restart takes two clicks; one restart per service every two minutes. The game server's restart warns players in Discord and in game for 10 minutes (the bot's status shows the countdown) before swapping and restarting; the in-game wrapper keeps its own 10-second countdown.
